Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India: Premium Design, Fast Charging & Pro-Level Camera Features at an Affordable Price

S Sayed Aminul
AlienWeb · Chauliabari, Bajali, Assam
Advertisement

The smartphone market in 2025 is hotter than ever, and one of the most talked-about launches this year is the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India. Packed with flagship-like features such as a 6.78-inch quad-curved AMOLED display, Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 processor, dual 50MP cameras, and a 6,500mAh battery, this phone aims to deliver a premium experience at a mid-range price.

But with so many options in the market — from iQOO to OnePlus and Xiaomi — buyers naturally have plenty of questions. Is it really worth buying? Does it support waterproofing? How good is the camera? Let’s break it down, question by question, so you know whether the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India is the right choice for you.

What is the price of Vivo T4 Pro

The official Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India is expected to launch at a price range between ₹25,000 – ₹30,000.

  • The 8GB + 256GB model is expected around ₹25,999.
  • The 12GB + 256GB model will cost close to ₹29,499.

This aggressive pricing makes it one of the best mid-range phones in 2025, directly challenging rivals like iQOO Z10 and OnePlus Nord 5 series.

What is the expected price of Vivo T4 in India

Apart from the Pro model, Vivo is also expected to launch a standard Vivo T4 at a slightly lower price. The expected price of the Vivo T4 in India is around ₹22,000 – ₹25,000, depending on the RAM and storage configuration.

So if you want to save a few thousand rupees and still enjoy the T-series performance, the base T4 might be a better fit.

Is Vivo T4 5G waterproof

Many users want to know if the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India comes with waterproofing. While Vivo has not officially confirmed an IP68 rating, the phone is designed to handle light splashes and dust resistance.

That means it’s fine for accidental water exposure, but it’s not recommended to fully submerge it like some flagship devices.

Is Vivo T4 5G good for gaming

Absolutely! The Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India is built with gamers in mind. With the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 processor, 144Hz refresh rate AMOLED display, and a 6,500mAh battery, the phone is capable of running popular games like BGMI, COD Mobile, and Genshin Impact smoothly at high settings.

The large battery combined with vapor cooling ensures that long gaming sessions won’t easily overheat the device.

Which is better, iQOO Z10 or Vivo T4

Both iQOO and Vivo are part of the same BBK Electronics family, but they focus on slightly different audiences. The iQOO Z10 is a pure gaming-centric device, while the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India balances both performance and design.

  • iQOO Z10 – better if you only care about gaming at high FPS.
  • Vivo T4 Pro – better if you want gaming + premium design + better cameras.

So overall, the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India gives you a more well-rounded experience.

Is Vivo T4 good for camera

Yes, camera lovers will appreciate the dual 50MP setup on the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India. It features:

  • 50MP OIS main sensor for sharp, stable images.
  • 50MP Sony IMX882 periscope telephoto lens with 3x optical zoom.
  • Aura ring light for night photography.
  • 32MP selfie camera for portraits and video calls.

For its price, the camera setup is one of the best in the segment.

Is Vivo T4 wireless charging

The Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India does not support wireless charging. Instead, it comes with 80W–90W super-fast wired charging, which is actually more useful in real life because it can charge the phone in less than an hour.

Additionally, you get 10W reverse charging to power small devices like earbuds.

Is Vivo phone a good phone

Yes, Vivo has steadily built its reputation for stylish designs, good cameras, and reliable hardware. With the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India, Vivo has once again proved that it can deliver premium features at affordable prices.

If you want a balance of performance, style, and durability, Vivo phones are a solid choice.

Is Vivo T4x worth buying

The Vivo T4x is expected to be a slightly toned-down version of the T4 Pro, targeting budget users. If you don’t need the periscope camera or the highest refresh rate, then yes, the Vivo T4x will be a value-for-money option.

But if you want the full premium experience, the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India is worth the extra spend.

Does Vivo heat up

Heating is a common question for smartphones. The Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India comes with a vapor cooling system, which helps keep the temperature under control during gaming or heavy multitasking.

Advertisement

Like any phone, it may get slightly warm, but it won’t overheat during normal use.

Which is best, Vivo T3x or Vivo T4x

The Vivo T3x was one of the popular mid-range launches in 2024, but the Vivo T4x (and T4 Pro) bring better specs like the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4, improved display, and larger battery.

So if you’re buying in 2025, the T4 series is clearly better.

Is the Vivo T4 curved display

Yes! One of the key highlights of the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India is its quad-curved AMOLED display. This makes it look premium and almost bezel-less, offering an immersive viewing experience.

Are Vivo and iQOO the same

Vivo and iQOO are both sub-brands of BBK Electronics. While Vivo focuses on design, cameras, and balanced performance, iQOO is mainly targeted at gamers.

So while they share technology, the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India is more about premium design and overall experience, whereas iQOO phones are gaming-first.

Does Vivo T4 have aura light

Yes, the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India includes Aura Ring Light, which enhances low-light and night photography. This is a unique feature that helps it stand out in its price range.

Does the Vivo T4 have Gorilla Glass protection

Yes, the display of the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India is protected by Gorilla Glass, making it resistant to scratches and minor drops. Combined with the slim glass design, it ensures durability without compromising looks.

Vivo T4 Pro 5G: Color Options

The Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India will be available in two stylish colors:

Nitro Blue – bold and modern.

Blaze Gold – classy and premium.

Both variants come with a glossy finish and Gorilla Glass protection, making them durable yet stylish.

Vivo T4 Pro 5G: U.S. Pricing & Availability

While the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India is grabbing attention in the Indian market, global buyers, especially in the United States, are also curious. The phone is expected to launch in the $399 – $449 price range in the U.S. Buyers can purchase it via the official Vivo U.S. Store and major retailers like Amazon and Best Buy once sales begin in September 2025.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. When will the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India be available for purchase?
The smartphone will be officially available in India from August 26, 2025, through Flipkart and select offline stores.

Q2. Does the Vivo T4 Pro 5G support 5G bands in India and the U.S.?
Yes, the device supports multiple 5G bands, ensuring fast connectivity both in India and international markets, including the United States.

Q3. How long does the Vivo T4 Pro battery last on heavy usage?
With a 6,500mAh battery, the phone can easily last more than a day on heavy gaming, video streaming, and multitasking.

Q4. Does the Vivo T4 Pro come with Android updates?
Yes, the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India will ship with Android 16 and is expected to receive at least three years of OS updates and four years of security patches.

Q5. What colors are available for the Vivo T4 Pro?
The device will be sold in Nitro Blue and Blaze Gold color options, giving users a stylish choice.

Q6. Does the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India support reverse charging?
Yes, it supports 10W reverse charging, so you can charge accessories like wireless earbuds directly from your phone.

Q7. Will the Vivo T4 Pro support Gorilla Glass protection on both sides?
Yes, the phone comes with Gorilla Glass protection on the front and back, adding durability alongside its curved display design.

Q8. Is the Vivo T4 Pro better than OnePlus Nord 5 series?
In terms of camera and battery life, the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India outperforms the OnePlus Nord 5. However, both are strong competitors in the ₹25K–₹30K segment.

Q9. Does the Vivo T4 Pro come with in-display fingerprint sensor?
Yes, the phone has an in-display fingerprint sensor, offering fast and secure unlocking.

Q10. Can I buy Vivo T4 Pro 5G on EMI in India?
Yes, Flipkart and offline partners are expected to offer no-cost EMI options, making it easier to buy the device.

Closing Thoughts on Vivo T4 Pro 5G

The Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India is proof that premium smartphone experiences don’t have to come with premium prices. With its flagship-like design, strong performance, excellent cameras, and massive battery, it is one of the most well-rounded devices under ₹30,000 in 2025.

For users looking for a stylish, powerful, and affordable smartphone, the Vivo T4 Pro 5G Unveiled in India is easily among the best choices available.
