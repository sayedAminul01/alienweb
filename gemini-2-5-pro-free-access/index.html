Google has launched an exclusive Gemini AI Pro free subscription offer for eligible college students in India. Valued at ₹19,500 annually, this premium AI suite is now free for 12 months to help students streamline their academic journey and gain Gemini 2.5 Pro free access.

This limited-time initiative is valid till September 15, 2025, and provides students with advanced tools like Gemini 2.5 Pro, NotebookLM with 5x limits, Veo 3, Gemini Live, and a whopping 2TB Google cloud storage.

  • Exam Preparation Tools – Practice questions, flashcards, step-by-step solutions
  • NotebookLM (Enhanced) – 5x higher limits for notebooks & source docs
  • Veo 3 Fast – AI-powered video generation using text/image input
  • Deep Research Tool – Academic research reports made easy
  • Audio Overviews – Turn materials into podcasts for learning on the go
  • Gemini Live – Real-time presentations, brainstorming, and creative writing
  • 2TB Cloud Storage – Across Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos
  • Seamless Integration – Gemini features in Gmail, Docs, Sheets & Slides

Why Gemini 2.5 Pro Free Access Is a Big Deal

Getting Gemini 2.5 Pro free access means unlocking Google’s most powerful AI to date. It can:

  • Solve complex academic problems
  • Generate code
  • Help you prepare for interviews
  • Support writing tasks with better logic and structure
  • Create personalized study materials

Students preparing for competitive exams, coding assessments, or academic research can benefit immensely from the AI’s contextual understanding and reasoning abilities.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for this offer:

  • You must be 18 years or older
  • Be enrolled in a recognized Indian college or university
  • Must provide either:
    • A valid college email ID, OR
    • Upload proof of enrollment (ID card, class schedule, or tuition receipt)

How to Get Gemini AI Pro Free for 1 Year – Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Visit the Gemini Student Offer Page

Go to gemini.google/students/?gl=IN and click on “Get Offer”.

Advertisement

Step 2: Verify Eligibility

Choose your college, fill in your name, date of birth, and either your .edu/college email or personal Gmail ID.

Step 3: Upload Proof (if needed)

If you’re using Gmail (non-college email), you’ll be asked to upload documents such as:

  • School or college ID card
  • Class schedule
  • Tuition fee receipt

Google uses SheerID for verification. It takes around 30 minutes.

Step 4: Add Payment Method (No Charge!)

Though you’ll see a ₹2000/month plan message, you won’t be charged for the first 12 months. Just add your UPI or card details for auto-renewal verification.

Important: Cancel AutoPay before your 12-month trial ends to avoid charges in 2026.

Step 5: Activation

Once verified, your Gemini AI Pro access activates. You now have Gemini 2.5 Pro free access, Google Vio3, Flow AI, and all the powerful tools built into Google Workspace (Gmail, Docs, Drive, etc.).
