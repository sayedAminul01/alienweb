Introduction

On July 28, 2025, Xiaomi publicly showed the Redmi Note 14 SE 5G in India, aiming to capture the low-cost smartphone market with flagship-level features at a reasonable cost. The newest model in the Redmi Note 14 lineup is likely to be a favourite among consumers seeking a high-performing, reasonably priced smartphone for less than ₹15,000.

Let’s explore the Redmi Note 14 SE 5G’s launch details, specifications, price in India, and key features in detail.
