iQOO 15 India Launch:A Brilliant Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 Flagship Delivering Unmatched Performance

S Sayed Aminul
AlienWeb · Chauliabari, Bajali, Assam
Advertisement

The flagship smartphone arena in India is a relentless battleground. Every year, brands push the envelope further, promising more power, better cameras, and sleeker designs. But with this innovation often comes a steep price tag, leaving consumers wondering if top-tier performance is always synonymous with a premium cost.

Scheduled for its grand unveiling on November 26, 2025, the iQOO 15 isn’t just another flagship entry. It is a strategic missile aimed directly at the heart of the competition. With a potential price shocker and a spec sheet that reads like a tech enthusiast’s wishlist, the iQOO 15 is poised to be the most disruptive smartphone launch of the year.

This isn’t mere speculation. Through exclusive sources and confirmed details, we have a comprehensive picture of what iQOO is bringing to the table. Buckle up, as we dive deep into the price, specifications, launch offers, and the potential market earthquake the iQOO 15 could trigger.

The Price Bomb: iQOO 15’s Most Potent Weapon

In an era where flagship prices are inching uncomfortably close to the one lakh rupee mark, iQOO seems to be playing a different game. According to multiple industry reports and insider information, the iQOO 15 India price is expected to be strategically positioned around Rs. 60,000 with limited-time launch offers.

This is a monumental claim. Let’s dissect what this means.

The “around Rs. 60,000” figure is likely the effective price after initial launch discounts and bank offers. The original price, excluding these promotions, is expected to be higher, potentially around the Rs. 65,000 to Rs. 69,900 mark, as hinted on the official website. However, by aggressively pushing an effective price point of ₹60,000, iQOO is sending a clear message to its rivals.

This pricing strategy could single-handedly crown the iQOO 15 as the most affordable smartphone in India to feature the next-generation Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 SoC. For consumers, this represents an unprecedented value proposition: getting bleeding-edge performance without completely obliterating their budget.

Why This Price Should Terrify the Competition

The timing of this launch is no coincidence. The smartphone market is bracing for the arrival of other flagships like the OnePlus 15 and the Realme GT 8 Pro. Industry whispers suggest these devices could be priced above Rs. 65,000 in India.

If iQOO holds true to its ₹60,000 launch offer price, it will undercut its direct competitors by a significant margin—potentially thousands of rupees. In the hyper-competitive Indian market, where value-for-money is a key purchasing driver, this price differential isn’t just a minor discount; it’s a disruptive feature.

For consumers torn between the OnePlus 15 and the iQOO 15, the latter’s aggressive pricing could be the decisive factor. It forces competitors to either re-evaluate their own pricing strategy or justify why their device is worth the extra premium. The iQOO 15, therefore, isn’t just a product; it’s a statement that could recalibrate the entire premium segment.

Don’t Miss Out: The iQOO 15 Priority Pass Explained

Understanding the importance of early adopters, iQOO has introduced an ingenious pre-booking system: the iQOO Priority Pass. This is your golden ticket to securing the device ahead of the crowd and bagging some incredible bonuses.

Here’s everything you need to know about the Priority Pass:

  • Cost: It can be purchased by paying a fully refundable Rs. 1,000. This isn’t an additional cost but a secure booking amount.
  • Availability: The pass will be available starting November 20, 2025, a week before the official launch. The company has explicitly stated that these passes are limited in number and will be sold on a strict first-come, first-served basis.
  • Exclusive Benefits: This is the best part. Priority Pass holders are entitled to two fantastic freebies:
    1. A complimentary iQOO TWS 1e wireless earbuds (worth approximately ₹1,899).
    2. A 12-month extended warranty on the device, providing extra peace of mind.

For anyone seriously considering the iQOO 15, the Priority Pass is a no-brainer. It costs nothing in the long run (as the ₹1,000 is refundable) and adds significant value to your purchase. Mark your calendars for November 20!

iQOO 15: A Technical Deep Dive into a Performance Powerhouse

A low price would mean nothing if the device itself wasn’t powerful. Fortunately, the iQOO 15’s specifications are anything but compromised. This is a true-blue flagship through and through.

Unmatched Performance: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and Beyond

At the core of the iQOO 15 lies the most anticipated chipset of 2025: the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5. Built on TSMC’s advanced 3nm process technology, this processor promises a monumental leap in efficiency and raw power. iQOO claims that this chipset delivers over a staggering 4 million points on the AnTuTu benchmark.

To put that into perspective, we are looking at a 20% improvement in single-core performance and a 23% boost in GPU capabilities compared to the previous generation. This translates to buttery-smooth UI navigation, instant app launches, and, most importantly, a transcendent gaming experience.

But iQOO didn’t stop there. The device is also powered by the brand’s in-house Supercomputing Q3 chip. This dedicated chip handles performance optimization, ensuring that the Snapdragon’s power is intelligently allocated for different tasks, whether it’s multitasking or an intense gaming session.

In terms of memory, the iQOO 15 is expected to launch in a single, top-tier variant featuring a massive 16GB of RAM and 512GB of internal storage. Some rumors even suggest a potential 1TB option, making it a future-proof beast for power users and content creators.

A Display That Dazzles: Samsung’s Finest OLED

iQOO has, for the first time, opted for a Samsung 2K M14 LEAD OLED display for its flagship. This isn’t just any screen; the company is boasting that it will be the brightest smartphone display in India, achieving a phenomenal 2,600 nits of peak brightness (HBM).

This insane brightness level ensures perfect visibility even under direct sunlight. Coupled with 2K resolution and support for Dolby Vision, every video, game, and image will pop with incredible color accuracy, deep blacks, and stunning contrast. The display is also expected to feature a silky-smooth 144Hz refresh rate, making scrolling and gameplay feel incredibly responsive.

Taming the Heat: India’s Largest Cooling System

A processor this powerful generates significant heat, especially during prolonged gaming. iQOO has addressed this with what it calls “India’s largest single-layer VC cooling system.” This vapor chamber boasts a colossal surface area of 8,000 mm².

This advanced cooling solution is designed to pull heat away from the SoC rapidly, preventing thermal throttling and maintaining peak performance for longer durations. For gamers, this means you can play the most graphically demanding titles at the highest settings without worrying about frame drops or lag caused by overheating.

Endurance Redefined: The 7000mAh Battery Beast

In a move that will resonate with every smartphone user, the iQOO 15 is confirmed to pack a massive 7000mAh battery. This isn’t just a large capacity; it’s a fourth-generation silicon anode battery, which typically offers higher energy density and better longevity than conventional cells.

This powerhouse is supported by incredibly fast charging. You get 100W wired fast charging that can presumably refill this giant battery in a remarkably short time. Furthermore, iQOO has also included 50W wireless fast charging support, adding a layer of convenience for users who prefer a cable-free experience. Say goodbye to battery anxiety.

Pro-Grade Photography: The Triple 50MP Camera Array

The iQOO 15 makes no compromises in the camera department. It sports a sophisticated triple-camera setup on the rear, and impressively, all three sensors are 50-megapixel units.

  • Primary Camera: A 50MP Sony VCS true color camera that aims to capture life-like colors with high fidelity.
  • Periscope Telephoto: A 50MP Sony 3x periscope telephoto lens that enables high-quality optical zoom. The device is also confirmed to include a 100x digital zoom capability (Tele X lens), allowing you to capture subjects from a great distance.
  • Ultra-Wide Sensor: A 50MP ultra-wide-angle sensor for capturing expansive landscapes and group shots. This sensor is also equipped with a dual-axis motor for rotation, potentially aiding in stabilization or specific shooting modes.

This versatile camera system, backed by capable software, positions the iQOO 15 as a serious contender for mobile photography enthusiasts. On the front, a high-resolution 32MP selfie camera is expected to handle video calls and self-portraits.

Software and Build: Long-Term Commitment and Ruggedness

The iQOO 15 will run on OriginOS 6, based on the latest Android 16 out of the box. More importantly, iQOO has made a commitment that is becoming a key differentiator in the market. The iQOO 15 will receive five years of major OS updates and seven years of security patches. This is a promise of long-term software support, ensuring your device remains secure and feature-rich for years to come.

Built to last, the phone also comes with both IP68 and IP69 certifications. This means it is not only protected against dust and immersion in water (IP68) but also against high-pressure, high-temperature water jets (IP69), making it one of the most durable flagships available.

iQOO 15 vs. The Competition: A New King in the Making?

The Indian premium smartphone segment is dominated by a few key players. Let’s see how the iQOO 15 stacks up against its anticipated rivals.

  • iQOO 15 vs. OnePlus 15: The OnePlus 15 will be its most direct competitor. Both will likely feature the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip. However, if iQOO’s aggressive pricing holds, it will have a significant advantage. The iQOO 15’s larger 7000mAh battery and the inclusion of a high-quality periscope telephoto lens could also be key differentiators.
  • iQOO 15 vs. Realme GT 8 Pro: The Realme GT series is known for its performance-centric approach, much like iQOO. The Realme GT 8 Pro will be a formidable foe. Again, the battle will come down to pricing, camera implementation, and unique software features. iQOO’s Priority Pass and bundled offers give it an early mindshare advantage.

10 Important iQOO 15 FAQs Answered

1. Is the iQOO 15 officially launched in India?
No, the iQOO 15 is not yet launched. The official launch date in India is November 26, 2025. Pre-bookings via the Priority Pass begin on November 20, 2025.

2. What is the price of the iQOO 15 Ultra?
As of now, there is no official information about an “iQOO 15 Ultra” model. All current reports and announcements focus solely on the standard iQOO 15. Any details about an Ultra variant are purely speculative.

3. Is there an iQOO 15 mini version coming to India?
No, iQOO has not announced any plans for a mini version of the iQOO 15. The brand appears to be focusing on a single, large-screen flagship model for this launch.

4. Which iQOO phone is the best?
While “best” can be subjective, the iQOO 15 is positioned to become the brand’s new flagship and best overall phone upon its release, thanks to the top-tier Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor, superior camera system, and massive battery.

5. Which is better, iQOO or OnePlus?
This depends on your priorities. iQOO often focuses on raw performance and gaming features at a competitive price. OnePlus has traditionally emphasized a clean software experience and premium build. With the iQOO 15’s aggressive pricing and top-end specs, it poses a serious challenge to the OnePlus 15, making it a very compelling alternative for performance seekers.

6. Does iQOO have heating issues?
iQOO has directly addressed this concern for the iQOO 15 by incorporating what it claims is “India’s largest single-layer VC cooling system” (8,000 mm²). This advanced cooling is specifically designed to manage the heat from the powerful chipset, which should significantly reduce the chances of overheating during intensive tasks compared to previous generations.

7. Is iQOO better than Vivo?
iQOO is actually a sub-brand of Vivo. iQOO’s focus is predominantly on high-performance and gaming-centric smartphones, often with a more aggressive design. Vivo’s main series often prioritizes camera innovation and design aesthetics. They cater to slightly different audiences within the same corporate umbrella.

8. Is Samsung better than iQOO?
Samsung offers a wide ecosystem, top-notch displays, and strong brand trust. iQOO, as a challenger brand, often provides more powerful hardware for the price. The iQOO 15, for instance, may offer a more powerful chipset than a similarly priced Samsung phone. The choice depends on whether you value brand ecosystem and software (Samsung) or pure performance and value (iQOO).

9. Is iQOO a Chinese company?
Yes, iQOO is a smartphone brand based in China. It was founded in 2019 as a sub-brand of the Chinese electronics manufacturer Vivo, which is itself a part of the BBK Electronics Corporation.

10. Is iQOO or Realme better?
Both brands are known for offering great value. iQOO typically leans harder into the gaming and performance segment. Realme offers a wider variety of devices across budgets. In the flagship segment, the iQOO 15 and Realme GT 8 Pro will be direct competitors. Based on initial specs, the iQOO 15 seems to have an edge with its larger battery and sophisticated camera system, but a final verdict requires a hands-on comparison.

The Final Verdict: Is the iQOO 15 Your Next Smartphone?

The iQOO 15 is shaping up to be a landmark device. It isn’t just incrementally better; it represents a strategic shift. It combines a no-compromise flagship specification sheet—from the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and 7000mAh battery to the triple 50MP cameras and brilliant display—with a price tag that aims to democratize flagship-level performance.

For gamers, it offers raw power and a superior cooling system. For multimedia consumers, it provides a best-in-class display and stellar battery life. For photography buffs, it presents a versatile and powerful camera setup. And for the value-conscious buyer, it delivers all of this at a price that seems almost too good to be true.

The launch on November 26, 2025, is set to be a watershed moment. If you are in the market for a premium smartphone, your decision just got a lot more interesting. The iQOO 15 has all the ingredients to not just compete but to dominate, forcing everyone else to up their game.

Our Advice? Set a reminder for November 20, 2025. Grab the Priority Pass from the official iQOO website or Amazon. Secure those free TWS earbuds and the extended warranty. The iQOO 15 isn’t just a phone; it’s an opportunity to experience the future of flagship performance, without the traditional flagship price. Don’t let this one slip away.
