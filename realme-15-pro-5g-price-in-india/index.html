Realme 15 Pro 5G Price in India, Full Specs, Amazing Features Updates

In India, the much awaited Realme 15 Pro 5G Price in India is scheduled to debut on July 24, 2025, at 7:00 PM IST. Realme is releasing the regular Realme 15 model in addition to the Pro version. After the debut, both phones will be sold on Flipkart, Realme.com, and other significant retail websites.

Everything you need to know about the Realme 15 Pro 5G Price in India, as well as its features, design, and specifications, is covered in detail in this article.

Price in India

The base model of the Realme 15 Pro 5G Price in India is expected to cost ₹35,000, while the top-end model will cost ₹39,999. The more affordable Realme 15 5G, which was designed for consumers on a tight budget, may launch for under ₹20,000.

Given its aggressive price, the Realme 15 Pro 5G Price in India can compete with high-end mid-range phones as the Samsung Galaxy A-series and OnePlus Nord series.

Display and Design

With its 6.8-inch curved AMOLED display, 1.5K resolution, and 144Hz refresh rate, the Realme 15 Pro 5G Price in India is a visual feast. With a maximum brightness of 6,500 nits, it is ideal for outdoor usage. The gadget is both IP68 and IP69 confirmed for dust and water protection, and Corning Gorilla Glass protects the display.

Highlights of the design:

  • A front camera with a punch-hole and thin bezels
  • Triple rear camera module in a square form
  • 7.69 mm in thickness
  • 187 kg in weight
  • Velvet green, flowing silver, and silk purple are the available colours

Performance

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset, which powers the Realme 15 Pro 5G Price in India, ensures the best performance for AI-driven tasks, gaming, and multitasking. It can support up to:

  • 12 GB of RAM
  • Internal storage of 512GB

In terms of software, it runs Android 15 with Realme UI 7, providing a clear, adaptable user interface with enhanced speed and privacy.

Battery

With its large 7,000mAh battery, the Realme 15 Pro 5G Price in India offers amazing long-term viability. This battery is reliable for gaming, binge-watching, and social media browsing.

Important features:

  • Fast charging with 80W wires
  • When fully charged, it can play music for up to 83 hours

camera setup

The smartphone has flagship-level capabilities thanks to its powerful triple-camera setup on the back:

  • The primary 50MP Sony IMX890 sensor features optical image stability (OIS)
  • Ultra-wide-angle 50MP camera
  • 60 fps 4K video recording

A 50MP selfie camera on the front is perfect for vloggers, content producers, and selfie lovers.

Colours

Three amazing finishes will be offered for the Realme 15 Pro 5G Price in India:

  • Green Velvet
  • Silver Flowing
  • Purple Silk

Velvet Green, Silk Pink, and Flowing Silver are the available colours for the regular Realme 15.

New Feature

Edge Genie, a voice-activated photo editing tool that employs AI to improve images with simple instructions, is a brand-new AI function that Realme 15 Pro 5G Price in India is teasing. This tool supports Realme’s 2025 action to integrate AI into both software and hardware.

Details of the Realme 15 Pro Launch Event

  • Time: 7:00 PM IST
  • Realme’s official YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram accounts are the places to watch

The brand will present the Realme 15 and Realme 15 Pro 5G Price in India, as well as detailed demos and pricing announcements, at the Realme 15 series launch event, which you can watch live.
