Why You Should Learn How to Recover Deleted Photos and Videos

What to Do Immediately After Deleting Photos or Videos

The first few minutes after deletion are critical. If you want the best chance of success while learning how to recover deleted photos and videos, follow these rules strictly:

  • Stop using the device immediately
  • Do not take new photos or videos
  • Avoid installing new apps
  • Turn off mobile data or Wi-Fi
  • Do not download files

These actions prevent overwriting deleted data.

Method 1: Recover Deleted Photos and Videos from Google Photos (Android)

Android users should always check Google Photos first. It keeps deleted media in the Trash folder.

Steps:

  1. Open Google Photos
  2. Go to Library → Trash
  3. Select photos or videos
  4. Tap Restore

Files stay for up to 60 days. This is one of the easiest ways to understand how to recover deleted photos and videos without third-party apps. For official guidance on restoring deleted media, users can refer to Google Photos Help documentation.

Method 2: Recover Deleted Photos and Videos on iPhone

Apple devices store deleted media temporarily in the Recently Deleted folder. iPhone users can also follow Apple’s official guide to recover deleted photos and videos from the Photos app.

Steps:

  1. Open Photos app
  2. Go to Albums → Recently Deleted
  3. Select files
  4. Tap Recover

For iPhone users, this method is a basic but powerful solution for how to recover deleted photos and videos. To unlock advanced smartphone settings and system-level features, readers can check Hidden Android Tricks in 2026, which explains powerful Android tips most users don’t know.

Method 6: Recover Deleted Photos and Videos from SD Card

If media was stored on an SD card:

Steps:

  1. Remove SD card from device
  2. Insert into card reader
  3. Connect to computer
  4. Scan using recovery software
  5. Restore files

Never save recovered files back to the same card. This rule is critical when applying how to recover deleted photos and videos correctly.

Method 7: Recover Deleted Photos and Videos After Factory Reset

Factory reset reduces recovery chances but does not always make recovery impossible.

Possible options:

  • Restore Google backup
  • Restore iCloud backup
  • Advanced PC recovery tools

This is a more difficult case of how to recover deleted photos and videos, but partial recovery is still possible in some situations.

Method 8: Recover Deleted Photos and Videos from WhatsApp

WhatsApp automatically backs up media.

Steps:

  1. Uninstall WhatsApp
  2. Reinstall and verify number
  3. Choose Restore from Backup

If backup was enabled, this is a practical way of understanding how to recover deleted photos and videos shared through chats.
