Redmi 15 5G Is launching in India, Amazing featuring, specifications, pricing details & the sale date.

S Sayed Aminul
AlienWeb · Chauliabari, Bajali, Assam
Advertisement

Xiaomi has expanded its popular Redmi lineup with the launch of the Redmi 15 5G in India on August 28, 2025. Celebrating 15 years of global innovation and 11 years in India, the company has introduced this affordable yet powerful smartphone to cater to budget-conscious buyers.

The Redmi 15 5G, which offers for less than ₹17,000 and has a stable 7000mAh battery, AI-powered features, and a flagship-inspired physical appearance, is a formidable rival in India’s challenging low-cost smartphone market. If you’re looking for a device that balances performance, display quality, and battery life without going over budget, this new model could be your next update.

Key Features of the Redmi 15 5G

Adaptive Sync 6.9-inch FHD+ display with a maximum refresh rate of 144 Hz

CPU: Qualcomm Snapdragon 6s Gen 3

Storage and RAM: 256GB of UFS 2.2 storage and 6GB/8GB of RAM (up to 16GB of virtual RAM can be added).

The back camera has a 50MP AI twin camera system.

An 8MP front-facing selfie camera

7000mAh battery with 33W rapid charging and 18W reverse

Operating System: Xiaomi HyperOS 2, based on Android 15.

Colors: Frosted White, Midnight Black, and Sandy Purple

In India, starting at ₹14,999

Display

The 6.9-inch FHD+ Adaptive Sync display on the Redmi 15 5G, with a refresh rate of 144 Hz, made it ideal for gamers, streamers, and binge-watchers. TÜV Rheinland’s triple certification minimizes eye strain and protects safe viewing for a long time.

The display offers quick touch performance and smooth images, giving customers a gaming-optimized and cinematic experience. It has touch sampling at 288 Hz and a maximum brightness of 850 nits.

Performance

The Redmi 15 5G is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 CPU that makes multitasking, gaming, and everyday tasks simple. When using UFS 2.2 storage and up to 8GB of RAM (which can be upgraded to 16GB via Virtual RAM), users may expect faster app loading times and more smooth performance.

As a result, the smartphone is a serious competitor to competitors like as the Poco M7 Plus 5G, which has similar performance but, on higher-end variants, only offers 128GB of storage.

Camera

The 50MP dual back camera system in the Redmi 15 5G comes with multiple AI functions, including: AI Erase: Remove unnecessary objects from images AI Sky: Enhance sky in pictures with smart changes AI Beauty Mode: Getting the best skin tone for gorgeous selfies To give images a classic, cinematic vibe, use vintage film filters. The 8MP front camera ensures sharp selfies and flexible video calls. Thanks to enhanced AI processing, flagship-level smartphone photography is now accessible to even low-budget customers.

 Battery and Charging

One of the Redmi 15 5G’s greatest features is its massive 7000mAh EV-grade battery with Si-C technology. Xiaomi claims to be able to offer: A maximum call duration of 55 hours Hold for 13.5 hours. Performance for frequent users all day long Because the smartphone supports 18W reverse charging and 33W fast charging (a charger comes in the package), it can power additional devices like wearables or earphones.
