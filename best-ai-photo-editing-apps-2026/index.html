Top 10 Best AI Photo Editing Apps 2026

Below is a complete list of the most advanced and user-friendly AI-powered editors available this year. Each of these apps offers unique features that make editing easier and more effective.

1. Remini – AI Photo Enhancer

Remini is one of the most widely used photo enhancement apps because of its ability to turn low-quality, blurry, or damaged photos into clear, high-resolution images. Many guides on the Best AI Photo Editing Apps 2026 list Remini as the top choice for photo restoration.

Key Features
  • HD enhancement
  • Old photo repair
  • AI face sharpening
  • Skin smoothing
Best For

Improving old, blurry, or low-resolution images.

2. PicsArt AI – Complete Editing Solution

PicsArt is a powerful and versatile editor offering creative AI effects, filters, background removal, and stylish retouching. It remains one of the Best AI Photo Editing Apps 2026 for social media users.

Key Features
  • AI-generated filters
  • Background changer
  • AI avatar maker
  • Beauty retouch
Best For

Beginners and creators who want fast, attractive edits.

3. Adobe Lightroom AI

Adobe Lightroom continues to dominate professional editing, and in 2026, it offers more advanced AI tools than ever. It is highly recommended in almost every Best AI Photo Editing Apps 2026 comparison list due to its powerful color correction and lighting tools.

Users who want to explore more professional AI editing features can also check the official Adobe website for advanced tools.

Key Features
  • AI color grading
  • Noise reduction
  • Curve adjustments
  • RAW file support
Best For

Photographers and advanced users who want professional results.

4. FaceApp AI – Portrait Retouching

FaceApp’s AI technology can transform facial expressions, fix imperfections, and adjust skin tone naturally. Many users include FaceApp among the Best AI Photo Editing Apps 2026 because of its unparalleled portrait editing accuracy.

Key Features
  • Facial reshaping
  • Age transformation
  • Skin smoothing
  • Expression adjuster
Best For

Portraits, selfies, and profile pictures.

5. Fotor AI Photo Editor

Fotor is known for its one-tap auto-enhance feature. It analyzes the image and adjusts lighting, sharpness, and color balance instantly. This app is also counted among the Best AI Photo Editing Apps 2026 due to its simplicity and effective results.

Key Features
  • AI enhancement
  • Background remover
  • Retouch tools
  • Artistic filters
Best For

Users who need quick and simple editing.

6. Snapseed AI

Snapseed by Google includes smart tools that analyze brightness, shadows, and colors to automatically adjust photos. It remains one of the top recommendations among the Best AI Photo Editing Apps 2026 for both beginners and semi-professionals. Key Features
  • Smart tune image
  • Healing tool
  • Portrait correction
  • Selective editing
Best For

Clean, sharp, and natural-looking edits.

7. Canva AI Photo Editor

Canva has become a leading editing and design platform. It helps users create posts, banners, posters, and thumbnails easily. Because of its AI-based background removal and auto-correction, it qualifies as one of the Best AI Photo Editing Apps 2026.

For additional AI-powered editing and design features, users can visit Canva’s official platform to explore more tools.

Key Features
  • Magic Eraser
  • AI background removal
  • Auto lighting adjustment
  • Design templates
Best For

Creators, students, and online businesses.

8. PhotoRoom – Clean Background Editing

PhotoRoom is one of the strongest competitors in the Best AI Photo Editing Apps 2026 list for background removal. It produces clean product photos that look professional.

Key Features
  • Accurate background cutout
  • White background mode
  • Product retouching
  • Batch editing
Best For

Product photos, online sellers, and branding.

9. Lensa AI – Magic Retouching

Lensa is known for face enhancement, AI avatars, and artistic effects. Many users include it in the Best AI Photo Editing Apps 2026 because it offers advanced beautification tools.

Key Features
  • Lighting adjustment
  • Skin smoothing
  • Portrait cleanup
  • AI avatar generator
Best For

Stylish and modern portrait editing.

10. Prisma AI – Artistic Photo Generator

Prisma converts regular photos into drawings, sketches, and paintings. Its unique art styles make it a popular choice among the Best AI Photo Editing Apps 2026.

Key Features
  • Artistic filters
  • Painting styles
  • Pencil sketch conversions
  • Creative effects
Best For

Art lovers and creative designers.
