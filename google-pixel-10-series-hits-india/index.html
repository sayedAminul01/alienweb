Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India with Stunning Pro AI Features and Bold Next-Gen Design: Starting at ₹79,999, Full Specs & Key Highlights

S Sayed Aminul
AlienWeb · Chauliabari, Bajali, Assam
Advertisement

For months, the smartphone world has been buzzing with leaks and whispers, but now it’s official — Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India. After a dazzling global launch in New York on August 20, 2025, Google brought the excitement straight to Indian fans with a dedicated event the very next day. Four brand-new models — Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold — were unveiled, each powered by the new Tensor G5 chip and loaded with Google’s most advanced AI features yet.

This launch feels different. Google isn’t just refreshing its lineup — it’s setting a new standard. The tagline “Ask more of your phone” isn’t just a catchy phrase; it reflects a shift toward phones that think, adapt, and help more than ever before. With smarter photography tools, a durable design, and AI-first performance, the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India as not just another flagship, but as Google’s strongest challenge to Apple and Samsung in years.

This year’s lineup is bigger than ever, featuring four models:

  • Pixel 10
  • Pixel 10 Pro
  • Pixel 10 Pro XL
  • Pixel 10 Pro Fold

All four models are powered by the new Tensor G5 chip, built on a 3nm process by TSMC. With improved efficiency, faster performance, and dedicated AI enhancements, Google is setting the stage for smartphones that are not just smart, but AI-first.

The tagline “Ask more of your phone” perfectly captures the spirit of this launch. Whether it’s Gemini Live, Magic Cue, or the all-new Camera Coach, Google wants you to rely on your Pixel more than ever.

And the cherry on top? The Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India with a starting price of ₹79,999, while U.S. buyers can get it starting at $899.

Design and Build Quality

When the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India, design is one of the first things users will notice. The series sticks to Google’s iconic camera bar design but gives it a modern refresh. The bar is now slimmer, stronger, and blends more smoothly with the polished aluminum frame.

  • Pixel 10: Compact, with flat edges and Gorilla Glass Victus 2 on both sides.
  • Pixel 10 Pro & Pro XL: Sleeker with curved-edge displays and thinner bezels.
  • Pixel 10 Pro Fold: Satin finish hinge, book-style folding design, and IP68 durability — the toughest foldable Google has ever built.

Color Options

Google has brought in fresh shades this year:
  • Pixel 10: Indigo, Frost, Lemongrass, Obsidian
  • Pixel 10 Pro: Jade, Moonstone, Obsidian, Porcelain
  • Pixel 10 Pro XL: Jade, Moonstone, Obsidian
  • Pixel 10 Pro Fold: Moonstone, Jade

The result? Whether you want something bold (Indigo, Jade) or classic (Obsidian, Porcelain), the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India with plenty of stylish choices.

Display Technology

Google has made huge improvements in display brightness and clarity this year. All models in the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India come with Super Actua OLED panels, which are some of the brightest and sharpest displays in the market.

  • Pixel 10: 6.3-inch OLED, 120Hz refresh rate, 3,000 nits peak brightness
  • Pixel 10 Pro: 6.3-inch LTPO OLED, adaptive 1–120Hz refresh, 3,300 nits peak brightness
  • Pixel 10 Pro XL: 6.8-inch LTPO OLED, adaptive refresh rate, 3,300 nits peak brightness
  • Pixel 10 Pro Fold: 6.4-inch cover display + 8-inch foldable Flex OLED, both with 120Hz refresh and 3,000 nits peak brightness

For Indian buyers, this is a big deal. Even under the scorching sun, the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India with displays that stay crystal clear outdoors.

Price in India and United States

Pricing is one of the most important factors for any flagship launch. With the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India, Google has made sure the phones remain premium but competitive.

India Pricing (256GB Variants)

  • Pixel 10: ₹79,999
  • Pixel 10 Pro: ₹1,09,999
  • Pixel 10 Pro XL: ₹1,24,999
  • Pixel 10 Pro Fold: ₹1,72,999

United States Pricing (256GB Variants)

  • Pixel 10: $899
  • Pixel 10 Pro: $1,099
  • Pixel 10 Pro XL: $1,199
  • Pixel 10 Pro Fold: $1,699

This dual pricing shows how Google is carefully balancing its flagship across markets. While India gets competitive pricing to fight Apple and Samsung, U.S. buyers get access to exclusive trade-in deals and store credits.

Availability in India

For Indian users, the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India with the following purchase details:

  • Pre-orders: Open immediately after launch
  • Sales Start: August 28, 2025
  • Retailers: Flipkart, Google Store, and select offline partners
  • Special Offer: Pixel 10 Pro and Pixel 10 Pro XL buyers get a free 1-year Google AI Pro subscription

Availability in the United States

For U.S. customers, the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India globally through:

  • Google Store (U.S.) – Click Here Official site with bundle offers and financing options
  • Major Retailers: Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart
  • Carriers: Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile (with promotions like up to $760 trade-in value or free gift cards)
  • Shipping Starts: Late August 2025

Google is also offering up to $350 store credits and trade-in deals for old Pixel or iPhone devices, making it more affordable for U.S. buyers.

Performance and Hardware

When the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India, one of its biggest talking points is performance. All four models are powered by Google’s brand-new Tensor G5 processor, developed in collaboration with TSMC on a 3nm process. This isn’t just a regular upgrade — it’s a leap in both speed and efficiency.

  • CPU Power: Up to 25% faster than last year’s Tensor G4
  • GPU Performance: Around 30% better for gaming and graphics-heavy tasks
  • AI Processing: Dedicated TPU (Tensor Processing Unit) for machine learning tasks
  • Efficiency: About 20% lower power usage

This means smoother gaming, faster multitasking, and a phone that can handle AI tasks without breaking a sweat.

Google has also added a vapor cooling chamber across all models, so whether you’re gaming on the Pixel 10 Pro XL or editing videos on the Pixel 10 Pro Fold, the phone won’t heat up as much.

Security is another highlight. The entire lineup ships with the Titan M2 security chip, which keeps your personal data safe from malware and cyber threats.

Camera System: The Triple Camera Era

When the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India, it brings a big camera upgrade — for the first time, every model in the lineup features a triple-camera setup.

Pixel 10 Camera Setup

  • 48MP main sensor
  • 13MP ultra-wide camera
  • 10.8MP telephoto lens with 5x optical zoom

This is a massive step up from the Pixel 9, especially for photography lovers who want zoom without sacrificing clarity.

Pixel 10 Pro and Pro XL Cameras

The Pro models raise the bar even higher:

  • 50MP primary sensor (better low-light shots)
  • 48MP ultra-wide lens
  • 48MP telephoto with 100x Pro Res Zoom

Selfies get a huge boost with a 42MP front camera — perfect for video calls, vlogs, and professional-quality selfies.

Pixel 10 Pro Fold Cameras

The Fold is no less impressive:

  • 48MP wide-angle camera
  • 10.5MP ultra-wide
  • 10.8MP telephoto lens
  • Dual 10MP selfie cameras (one on the cover display, one inside)

Whether it’s group selfies, landscapes, or long-distance zoom, the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India with cameras that easily compete with the iPhone 16 Pro Max and Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra.

Battery and Charging

Battery life has always been important for Indian users, and this year Google has improved things further.

  • Pixel 10: 4,970mAh, 30W wired charging, 15W Qi2 wireless charging
  • Pixel 10 Pro: 4,870mAh, 30W wired charging, wireless support
  • Pixel 10 Pro XL: 5,200mAh, 45W wired fast charging, 25W wireless charging
  • Pixel 10 Pro Fold: 5,015mAh, 30W wired + 15W wireless

In simple words — no matter which model you buy, you’ll easily get a full day of use, even with heavy AI features running. The Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India with reliable battery performance, backed by Google’s efficient Tensor G5 chip.

Software: Android 16 and Beyond

All models in the series ship with Android 16 out of the box. But what makes the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India even more special is Google’s promise of seven years of OS and security updates. That’s industry-leading and even beats Apple in some areas.

Google also continues the tradition of Pixel-exclusive features, which usually drop in “Pixel Feature Drops” every few months. Buyers can expect new AI features, camera improvements, and productivity tools regularly.

AI Features: The Real Game-Changer

The real reason the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India feels different from other phones is AI. With the Tensor G5, Google has supercharged how AI works on your device.

Here are the key AI features:

  • Camera Coach: Guides you in real-time while taking photos, helping you frame and capture better shots.
  • Gemini Live: Google’s conversational AI built into the phone — think of it as a smarter assistant that can answer, summarize, and even hold real-time conversations.
  • Magic Cue: Offers smart suggestions across apps, whether it’s email replies, calendar planning, or photo edits.
  • Auto Best Take: Lets you select the best face from multiple shots, so group photos look perfect.
  • Pro Controls & High-Res Mode (Pro models only): Full manual controls for photography lovers.

These features make the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India not just another phone launch, but the beginning of a new AI-first era.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: When did the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India officially?
The Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India on August 21, 2025, just a day after its global launch event in New York.

Q2: What is the starting price of the Pixel 10 series in India?
The series starts at ₹79,999 for the Pixel 10 model. Premium models like the Pixel 10 Pro XL and Pixel 10 Pro Fold are priced higher.

Q3: How much does the Google Pixel 10 Series cost in the U.S.?
In the U.S., the lineup begins at $899 for the Pixel 10 and goes up to $1,699 for the Pixel 10 Pro Fold.

Q4: Where can I buy the Google Pixel 10 Series Hits India?
Indian buyers can get the phones through Flipkart, Google Store, and major offline retailers. Sales begin from August 28, 2025.

Q5: Where is the Pixel 10 series available in the U.S.?
In the U.S., the phones are available on the Google Store, Amazon, Best Buy, and leading carriers like Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile.

Q6: Which processor powers the Pixel 10 series?
All models run on the new Google Tensor G5 processor, designed for AI-driven performance and efficiency.

Q7: What makes the cameras in the Pixel 10 series special?
Each model comes with a triple-camera system, while the Pro and Pro XL feature 100x Pro Res Zoom for next-level photography.

Q8: How long will the Pixel 10 series receive updates?
