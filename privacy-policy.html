Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AlienWeb ("we", "us", "AlienWeb.in") handles information when you use our website. We built AlienWeb to be as privacy-friendly as possible — most tools process your input entirely in your browser and never send it to a server.

1. Information we do not collect

The text, numbers, files, or other input you type or paste into any AlienWeb tool (word counter, JSON formatter, password generator, calculators, etc.) is processed locally in your browser using JavaScript. We do not transmit, store, or have access to that content.

2. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our server and third-party services (such as Google AdSense) may automatically log standard technical information when you visit, including your approximate location (derived from IP address), browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring URLs. This is typically used in aggregate for analytics and ad delivery, not to identify you personally.

3. Cookies and Google AdSense

AlienWeb displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. This helps show you more relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies used for personalization by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

4. Children's privacy

AlienWeb is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

5. Data security

Because tool input is processed client-side and not transmitted to our servers, there is inherently minimal server-side data to secure. Any communication you send us directly (e.g. via email) is handled at your discretion and stored only as long as needed to respond.

6. Third-party links

Our site may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party sites. Please review their privacy policies directly.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our Contact page.
