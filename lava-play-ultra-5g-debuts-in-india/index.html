Lava Play Ultra 5G Debuts in India – Powerful Gaming Phone Under ₹15,000

S Sayed Aminul
AlienWeb · Chauliabari, Bajali, Assam
Advertisement

Lava Play Ultra 5G Debuts in India on August 20, 2025, bringing gamers and budget smartphone buyers an exciting option under ₹15,000. The device comes with a MediaTek Dimensity 7300 chipset, a 120Hz AMOLED display, a 64MP Sony OIS camera, and a bloatware-free Android 15 experience.

By offering flagship-like features at a budget price, Lava is positioning the Play Ultra 5G as a direct competitor to brands like Xiaomi, Realme, and iQOO in India’s competitive 5G smartphone market.

Lava Play Ultra 5G: Key Specifications

When the Lava Play Ultra 5G Debuts in India, it comes with the following specifications:

  • Display: 6.67-inch AMOLED FHD+ display, 120Hz refresh rate, 1000 nits peak brightness, 100% DCI-P3 color gamut
  • Processor: MediaTek Dimensity 7300 (4nm), Mali-G615 GPU with HyperEngine
  • RAM & Storage: 6GB/8GB LPDDR4x RAM + 128GB UFS 3.1 storage, expandable up to 1TB
  • Virtual RAM: Up to 8GB additional
  • Rear Cameras: 64MP Sony IMX682 with OIS + 5MP macro lens
  • Front Camera: 13MP selfie shooter
  • Battery: 5000mAh with 33W fast charging
  • Software: Android 15, 2 OS upgrades + 3 years of security updates
  • Other Features: In-display fingerprint sensor, stereo speakers, IP64 water/dust resistance
  • Colors: Arctic Frost, Arctic Slate

Display and Design

The Lava Play Ultra 5G Debuts in India with a 6.67-inch AMOLED screen offering a 120Hz refresh rate and up to 1000 nits brightness, making it ideal for gaming and multimedia. With 10-finger multi-touch support, stereo speakers, and an oleophobic coating, it enhances both usability and durability.

Performance

The Lava Play Ultra 5G Debuts in India powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 7300 chipset, delivering smooth multitasking and gaming. With HyperEngine optimization, it offers up to 20% higher frame rates and better efficiency.

Paired with up to 8GB RAM (expandable with virtual RAM) and UFS 3.1 storage, this phone ensures fluid performance whether gaming, streaming, or multitasking.

Camera Capabilities

The Lava Play Ultra 5G Debuts in India with a 64MP Sony IMX682 OIS camera and a 5MP macro lens at the rear. The front hosts a 13MP selfie camera.

It supports multiple shooting modes including Night Mode, HDR, Panorama, Pro Video, Dual View Video, Macro, and Time Lapse, making it versatile for content creators and casual photographers alike.

Battery and Charging

Packed with a 5000mAh battery, the Lava Play Ultra 5G Debuts in India as a reliable all-day performer. With 33W fast charging, the device can go from 0–100% in about 83 minutes.

Lava claims up to 45 hours of talk time, 510 hours standby, and nearly 650 minutes of YouTube playback, catering well to heavy users.

Software and Updates

The Lava Play Ultra 5G Debuts in India running on clean Android 15, free from bloatware and ads. Lava guarantees 2 years of OS updates and 3 years of security patches, ensuring long-term usability and security.

This makes it one of the cleanest and most reliable Android experiences in its price range.

Price and Availability

The Lava Play Ultra 5G Debuts in India in two storage variants:

  • 6GB + 128GB – ₹14,999 (₹13,999 with bank offers)
  • 8GB + 128GB – ₹16,499 (₹15,499 with bank offers)
  • Launch Date: August 20, 2025
  • First Sale: August 25, 2025 (Amazon exclusive)

Available in Arctic Frost and Arctic Slate, buyers using ICICI, SBI, or HDFC Bank cards can avail ₹1,000 instant discount. Additionally, Lava provides Free Service@Home support pan-India.
