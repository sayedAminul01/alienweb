How to use the Lorem Ipsum generator

  1. Choose whether you want output measured in paragraphs, sentences, or words.
  2. Enter how many of that unit you need.
  3. Leave the checkbox on to start with the traditional "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet" opening, or uncheck it for fully random text.
  4. Click Generate, then copy the result into your design tool, CMS, or document.

Why use a Lorem Ipsum generator?

Real content usually isn't ready when a design or layout needs to be reviewed, so designers and developers fill the space with Lorem Ipsum — scrambled Latin-derived text that looks like natural language without carrying any real meaning. That neutrality is the point: reviewers focus on typography, spacing, and hierarchy instead of getting distracted by actual words. This generator builds fresh, randomized Lorem Ipsum on the fly using a real classical word bank, so no two blocks of text look identical, and you control exactly how much you need in paragraphs, sentences, or words.

What is Lorem Ipsum text used for?

Lorem Ipsum is scrambled, meaningless Latin-derived text used by designers and developers as placeholder copy so layouts can be reviewed without real content distracting from the design.

Can I generate lorem ipsum by words, sentences, or paragraphs?

Yes. Choose the unit you need from the dropdown, set a count, and click Generate to get exactly that many words, sentences, or paragraphs.

Why does the generator start with 'Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet'?

That opening phrase is the traditional start of Lorem Ipsum text. You can leave the checkbox on for the classic look or turn it off for fully randomized text.
