Tecno Pova Slim 5G Mobile Arive In India Ultra-Thin Premium Attractive Design, AI Features, Price & Full Review 2025

S Sayed Aminul
AlienWeb · Chauliabari, Bajali, Assam
Advertisement

Smartphone brands are always trying to push boundaries with slimmer designs, faster performance, and smarter features. In 2025, Tecno Pova Slim 5G Mobile entered the market with a bold promise — to be one of the slimmest and most stylish 5G phones, without compromising on power.

With just 5.95mm thickness and 156g weight, this phone feels feather-light yet durable. Add a 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED display, MediaTek Dimensity 6400 processor, 5160mAh battery, and Ella AI features, and the result is a mid-range phone that rivals many flagships.

This article covers everything about the Tecno Pova Slim 5G Mobile — specs, design, performance, battery, cameras, AI features, price, availability, and answers to the most common questions buyers ask.

Tecno Pova Slim 5G Mobile: Key Highlights

  • Thickness: 5.95mm ultra-thin
  • Weight: 156 grams
  • Display: 6.78-inch 3D curved AMOLED, 144Hz refresh rate, 240Hz touch sampling
  • Peak Brightness: 4,500 nits
  • Processor: MediaTek Dimensity 6400
  • RAM & Storage: 8GB RAM + 128GB storage (UFS 2.2)
  • Battery: 5160mAh with 45W fast charging
  • Rear Cameras: 50MP + 2MP dual setup
  • Front Camera: 13MP
  • OS: HiOS 15 based on Android 15
  • Audio: Single speaker with Dolby Atmos
  • Connectivity: Dual SIM 5G, Infrared sensor
  • Durability: Gorilla Glass 7i, IP64 splash resistance, TÜV Rheinland certification
  • AI Features: Ella AI (call assistant, writing, image editing, privacy blurring)
  • Price: ₹19,999 (8GB + 128GB)
  • Launch Date: September 4, 2025
  • Availability: Sale from September 8, 2025 via Flipkart & offline stores

Design and Build Quality

The Tecno Pova Slim 5G Mobile is designed for people who hate bulky phones but still want performance. With its 5.95mm profile and 156g weight, it’s slimmer and lighter than most competitors. Despite this, it doesn’t feel fragile, thanks to:

  • Gorilla Glass 7i protection
  • IP64 water and dust resistance
  • Aluminum side frame

It comes in Cool Black, Slim White, and Sky Blue, all with a glossy yet durable finish. This makes it not just a smartphone, but a style statement.

Display: AMOLED with 144Hz Refresh Rate

The Tecno Pova Slim 5G Mobile features a 6.78-inch curved AMOLED display that supports 144Hz refresh rate and 240Hz touch sampling. This is perfect for smooth animations, faster touch response, and immersive visuals.

With a peak brightness of 4,500 nits, outdoor readability is excellent, while Dolby Atmos support improves the multimedia experience. Whether it’s gaming, movies, or browsing, this display feels premium.

Performance: Powered by MediaTek Dimensity 6400

Inside, the Tecno Pova Slim 5G Mobile runs on the MediaTek Dimensity 6400 processor, a 5G-enabled chipset built on 6nm technology. It offers:

  • Faster app load times
  • Smooth multitasking
  • Gaming-friendly performance

Paired with 8GB RAM and 128GB UFS 2.2 storage, this smartphone can easily handle gaming, streaming, and multitasking without lag.

Battery and Charging

Even with its slim profile, the Tecno Pova Slim 5G Mobile houses a 5160mAh battery — larger than many slim competitors. It supports 45W fast charging:

  • 0–50% in 25 minutes
  • Full charge in ~55 minutes

For a phone this slim, Tecno has done a great job balancing design and endurance.

Cameras and AI Imaging

The Tecno Pova Slim 5G Mobile comes with:

  • 50MP primary sensor
  • 2MP secondary lens
  • 13MP front camera

AI tools like Ella AI, AI Image Editing, Privacy Blurring, and Super HDR enhance images and videos. While it doesn’t have a triple-lens setup, AI ensures balanced results for social media and everyday photography.

Software and AI Features

The device runs on HiOS 15 (based on Android 15) with improved customization and smooth navigation.

The highlight is Ella AI, Tecno’s in-house assistant that supports multiple Indian languages. Features include:

  • AI Call Assistant (blocks spam & manages calls)
  • AI Writing (helps draft smarter messages)
  • AI Image Editing
  • Privacy Blurring (for safe image sharing)

This makes the Tecno Pova Slim 5G Mobile more than just hardware — it’s smart, personal, and user-friendly.

Price and Availability

The Tecno Pova Slim 5G Mobile is priced at ₹19,999 for the 8GB + 128GB variant.

  • First Sale: September 8, 2025
  • Where to Buy: Flipkart and authorized offline retail stores
