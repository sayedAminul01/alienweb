Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60 || The Ultimate Mid-Range Showdown – Which One Reigns Supreme in 2025?

S Sayed Aminul
AlienWeb · Chauliabari, Bajali, Assam
Advertisement

In the ever-evolving world of mid-range smartphones, Vivo’s V-series has carved out a niche for itself with stylish designs, reliable cameras, and unbeatable battery life. But with the launch of the Vivo V60e 5G and the established Vivo V60, choosing between them can feel like a tough call. Both phones promise a premium experience at accessible prices, but they cater to slightly different needs. The Vivo V60e 5G brings a massive 200MP camera and efficient MediaTek power, while the Vivo V60 flexes Zeiss optics and Snapdragon muscle.

Whether you’re a photography enthusiast eyeing zoom capabilities or a gamer hunting for smooth performance, this head-to-head comparison will break down every detail. From camera versatility to battery endurance, we’ll explore what’s new, what’s improved, and what’s worth your hard-earned cash in India. As we dive into October 2025’s hottest Vivo duo, get ready to discover which one truly elevates your daily digital life. Let’s get started!

Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60: A Quick Overview of the Contenders

Picture this: You’re scrolling through endless options on Flipkart or Amazon, and Vivo’s V60 lineup catches your eye. The Vivo V60e 5G, Vivo’s latest budget-friendly entrant, hits the sweet spot for value seekers. Priced starting at ₹29,999, it packs a punch with its 200MP sensor and slim design. On the flip side, the Vivo V60, at ₹36,999, targets those craving pro-level photography and raw power.

Both share Vivo’s signature flair – vibrant AMOLED screens, massive batteries, and that signature Aura Light for low-light magic. But under the hood? That’s where the real drama unfolds. The V60e opts for affordability with compromises on zoom, while the V60 goes all-in on versatility. If you’re upgrading from an older mid-ranger like the Vivo V50, these could be game-changers. Stay tuned as we unpack the nuances that make each shine – or stumble.

In India’s bustling smartphone market, where competition from Samsung’s A-series and Nothing’s mid-rangers rages on, Vivo’s strategy is clear: Balance style with substance. But which model delivers the best bang for your buck? Let’s zoom in on the cameras first – because let’s face it, that’s where most of us snap our decisions.

Camera Clash: Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60 – Pixels, Zoom, and Zeiss Magic

Cameras are the heart of Vivo’s V-series appeal, and the Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60 battle here is fierce. The Vivo V60e 5G boasts a standout 200MP main sensor – a 1/1.56-inch beast that’s all about detail in broad daylight. Paired with an 8MP ultrawide, it captures expansive landscapes without breaking a sweat. But here’s the kicker: No Zeiss branding means you’re missing out on those signature color tunings and bokeh effects that make portraits pop.

Flip to the Vivo V60, and it’s a photographer’s dream. The 50MP Sony IMX766 main sensor with OIS delivers crisp, natural shots, but the real star is the 50MP 3x periscope telephoto lens. Zoom in on distant subjects – be it a cricket match or a city skyline – and you’ll see why Zeiss collaboration elevates everything. The 8MP ultrawide rounds out a triple-camera setup that’s versatile for vlogs, selfies, and everything in between.

Selfies? Both rock a 50MP front camera on a 1/2.76-inch sensor, supporting 4K video for TikTok-worthy clips. In low light, the Vivo V60’s Aura Light ring edges ahead, filling shadows with soft, flattering illumination. But the V60e’s 200MP punch shines in well-lit scenarios, churning out poster-sized prints without pixelation.

Real-world testing reveals the trade-offs. The Vivo V60e 5G excels in casual snaps – family gatherings, street food hauls – but struggles with zoom beyond 2x, relying on digital crop that softens details. The Vivo V60? It nails portraits with Zeiss Biotar and Cinematic modes, though its ultrawide falls flat in dim conditions, producing noisy edges.

For content creators, the Vivo V60’s video chops impress with 4K 30fps stabilization across main and telephoto lenses. The V60e matches on the main but caps ultrawide at 1080p – a minor nitpick for most. If photography is your jam, the Vivo V60 pulls ahead; for budget snappers, the V60e 5G keeps it simple and sharp.

As we move beyond lenses, remember: Vivo’s cameras aren’t just hardware – they’re software-savvy too. AI enhancements like scene detection and edge detection make both phones smarter shooters. But in this Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60 lens, versatility crowns the V60 king.

Power Under the Hood: Chipset Showdown in Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60

Performance is where mid-range phones separate the wheat from the chaff, and the Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60 chipset duel doesn’t disappoint. The Vivo V60e 5G runs on the MediaTek Dimensity 7300 Turbo – a solid 4nm chip that’s efficient for daily multitasking. Paired with up to 12GB RAM and 256GB UFS storage, it handles social scrolls, light editing, and casual gaming like a champ. AnTuTu scores hover around 700,000, making it snappy for the price.

Enter the Vivo V60 with its Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 – Qualcomm’s upper-mid-tier powerhouse on a 4nm process. Clocking AnTuTu north of 900,000, it breezes through heavy apps, 4K edits, and high-frame-rate games like BGMI at 90fps. With up to 16GB RAM and 512GB storage, multitasking feels effortless, even with a dozen tabs open.

Base models align at 8GB/128GB, but the V60’s higher ceiling suits power users. In Geekbench, the Snapdragon edges out with single-core scores ~1,200 vs the Dimensity’s ~1,000, translating to quicker app launches. Gaming? The V60 sustains longer sessions without throttling, thanks to a larger vapor chamber.

Thermals are managed well on both, but the V60e’s efficiency shines in battery sips during idle. For students or commuters, the V60e 5G suffices; gamers and creators? The V60’s Snapdragon supremacy wins.

Software layers Funtouch OS 15 on Android 15 for both, promising 4 years of OS updates and 6 years of security. But Vivo’s bloatware – preloaded apps and aggressive RAM management – can irk. The V60 feels more polished, with smoother animations.

In this Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60 performance face-off, the Snapdragon tips the scales – but at a premium price.

Display Duel: Brightness, Curves, and Crispness in Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60

Nothing beats a vibrant screen for binge-watching or doom-scrolling, and both Vivo models deliver with 6.77-inch AMOLED panels at 120Hz refresh. Peak brightness hits 5,000 nits on either, banishing glare on sunny Indian streets. HDR10+ support means Netflix sessions pop with color and contrast.

Resolution? Both stick to FHD+ (1080p) at 388 PPI – sharp enough for most, but a step down from the V50’s 1.5K. Curved edges on the V60 add immersion for media, but increase accidental touches. The V60e 5G’s flat display feels more practical for typing and gaming.

Touch sampling at 480Hz ensures responsive swipes, and adaptive refresh saves juice. Both pack under-display fingerprint scanners that are speedy and secure.

Downgrades sting: Compared to the V50’s sharper panel, these feel regressive. Outdoor visibility is stellar, but fine text in e-books lacks punch.

For movie buffs, the V60’s curves enhance; productivity pros prefer the V60e 5G’s flat real estate. In display terms, it’s a tie – solid, but not spectacular.

Battery Battle: Endurance and Speed in Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60

Battery life is Vivo’s secret weapon, and both phones wield a 6,500mAh titan. Expect 10-12 hours of screen time on mixed use – streaming, calls, and light gaming. Heavy users squeeze a full day; standby drain is minimal.

90W FlashCharge is identical: 0-50% in 25 minutes, full in under an hour. Bundled adapters make it hassle-free, and reverse charging powers earbuds on the go.

The V60’s Snapdragon sips more efficiently under load, edging a slight lead in gaming marathons. Both support USB-C 2.0, but no wireless charging – a miss at this price.

In real tests, neither overheats during charges, thanks to smart thermal limits. For travelers or all-day warriors, these are endurance champs.

Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60 battery? Dead heat – massive capacity meets rapid refills.

Design and Build: Style Meets Durability in Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60

Vivo’s design ethos shines: Slim profiles (7.5-7.8mm) bely the hefty battery. The V60e 5G in Moonlit Blue or Mist Gray feels premium, with a flat frame that’s pocket-friendly. The V60 adds Auspicious Gold, but its glossy sides scratch easily – case included helps.

IP68/IP69 ratings mean both survive swims or dust storms – rare for mid-rangers. Schott glass fronts add scratch resistance, but curved V60 screen risks cracks.

Camera bumps are pill-shaped with Aura Light – subtle yet effective. Weights hover at 190-200g, balanced for one-hand use.

The V60’s Zeiss logo screams luxury; V60e keeps it understated. Both feel solid, but V60e’s flat display wins ergonomics.

In aesthetics, Vivo nails it – stylish without sacrificing build.

Price and Value: Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60 in India – Worth the Splurge?

Pricing seals the deal. Vivo V60e 5G starts at ₹29,999 (8/128GB), topping ₹33,999 (12/256GB). Vivo V60? ₹36,999 base, up to ₹42,999 for maxed-out configs.

At Flipkart or Vivo stores, expect EMI, exchanges, and festive deals. V60e offers 80% V60 features for 75% price – ideal for budget buyers.

V60 justifies premium with telephoto and Snapdragon. But UFS 2.2 lag and display downgrade question value.

For most Indians, V60e 5G steals value crown.

Software and Updates: Funtouch OS on Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60

Funtouch OS 15 brings Android 15 fluidity to both. Custom icons, themes, and Aqua Dynamics smooth transitions.

Bloat persists – duplicate apps clutter. AI like Circle to Search shines, but needs internet.

4 OS + 6 security years? Vivo’s best yet. V60 feels optimized; V60e matches closely.

Solid, but not stock Android clean.

Verdict: Vivo V60e 5G vs Vivo V60 – Pick Your Winner

V60e 5G: Budget king for cameras, battery. V60: Pro choice for zoom, power.

Choose based on needs – both elevate Vivo’s mid-range game.
