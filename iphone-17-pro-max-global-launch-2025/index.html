One of the biggest highlights of the iPhone 17 Pro Max Global Launch is its triple 48MP camera setup. Apple has gone all-in on computational photography and hardware upgrades:

  • 48MP Wide Camera with OIS – Perfect for everyday shots, sharper and brighter even in low light.
  • 48MP Ultra-Wide Lens – Expanded field of view, improved distortion correction for landscapes.
  • 48MP Periscope Telephoto – 6x optical zoom, bringing DSLR-like detail into a phone.

On the video side:

  • 8K Cinematic Recording at 30fps.
  • Cinematic Mode 2.0 with advanced depth control.
  • Apple ProRAW & ProRes for professionals.

Even the 12MP TrueDepth front camera has been enhanced with AI portrait recognition and low-light selfies.

Verdict: For photography lovers, the iPhone 17 Pro Max Global Launch sets a new gold standard.
