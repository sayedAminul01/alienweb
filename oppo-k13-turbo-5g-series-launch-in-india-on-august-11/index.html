Oppo K13 Turbo 5G Series Launch in India on August 11, 2025 With Exciting Price, Features, and Specs Shared

S Sayed Aminul
AlienWeb · Chauliabari, Bajali, Assam
Advertisement

The Oppo K13 Turbo 5G series, including the K13 Turbo and K13 Turbo Pro, is set to launch in India on August 11, 2025, bringing flagship-level features to the mid-range segment. Priced under Rs. 40,000, this series introduces India’s first inbuilt cooling fan, robust processors, and a massive 7,000mAh battery, making it a top pick for gamers and tech enthusiasts. Exclusively available on Flipkart, here’s everything you need to know about the Oppo K13 series.

Launch Date and Expected Price

Oppo will unveil the K13 Turbo series on August 11, 2025, targeting India’s mid-range smartphone market. Based on China’s pricing, the K13 Turbo may start at approximately Rs. 21,500 (Yuan 1,799), with the top variant at Rs. 27,500 (Yuan 2,299). The K13 Turbo Pro is expected to range from Rs. 24,000 (Yuan 1,999) to Rs. 32,500 (Yuan 2,699). Official Indian prices and storage variants will be confirmed at the launch event.

Revolutionary Cooling Technology

The Oppo K13 series stands out with India’s first inbuilt cooling fan, spinning at up to 18,000 RPM to manage heat during intense tasks like gaming. Paired with a 7,000 sq mm vapour chamber and a 19,000 sq mm graphite layer, this cooling system ensures smooth performance without overheating, making it ideal for power users.

Sleek Design and Vibrant Colors

The K13 Turbo series combines performance with style, available in Silver Knight, Purple Phantom, and Midnight Maverick. The K13 Turbo Pro features a Turbo Breathing Light with dual Mist Shadow LEDs and eight-color RGB lighting around the camera island, giving it a premium, futuristic look. The standard K13 Turbo sports a Turbo Luminous Ring that glows softly in the dark after exposure to UV or sunlight. The K13 Turbo also offers an exclusive White Knight variant for added flair.

High-Performance Processors

The K13 Turbo is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 8450 chipset with a Mali G720 MC7 GPU, paired with up to 16GB LPDDR5X RAM and 1TB UFS 3.1 storage for seamless multitasking and gaming. The K13 Turbo Pro, equipped with the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 processor (also in the iQOO Neo 10), offers up to 16GB LPDDR5X RAM and 1TB UFS 4.0 storage, ensuring faster app performance and data speeds.

Immersive Display and Long-Lasting Battery

The K13 series features a 1.5K Ultra HD display with a 120Hz refresh rate, delivering vibrant visuals and smooth scrolling for gaming and streaming. It includes low blue light technology to reduce eye strain. Both models are backed by a 7,000mAh battery with 80W SuperVOOC fast charging, offering all-day power and quick recharges.

Camera for Content Creators

The Oppo K13 series is expected to feature a 50MP dual-camera setup on the rear, capturing detailed photos and videos. A 16MP front camera is likely to handle selfies and video calls, making it perfect for social media enthusiasts.

Why Choose the Oppo K13 Series?

With its innovative cooling fan, powerful processors, and stylish design, the Oppo K13 series competes with mid-range rivals like the iQOO Neo 10 and Realme GT series. Priced under Rs. 40,000, it offers flagship-level performance for gamers and budget-conscious buyers. Its exclusive availability on Flipkart ensures easy access for Indian consumers.

Where to Purchase

The Oppo K13 Turbo and K13 Turbo Pro will be available exclusively on Flipkart, India’s leading e-commerce platform, starting post-launch on August 11, 2025. Flipkart’s exclusive partnership with Oppo ensures easy access, with potential offers like no-cost EMI, exchange discounts, and bank deals. Visit Flipkart’s official website or app to pre-order or purchase the K13 series and stay updated on launch-day promotions.

FAQs Of Oppo K13 Turbo series

  1. When will the Oppo K13 Turbo series launch in India?
    The K13 Turbo and Turbo Pro will launch on August 11, 2025.
  2. What is the expected price of the Oppo K13 series?
    The K13 Turbo may start at Rs. 21,500, and the K13 Turbo Pro at Rs. 24,000. Final pricing will be announced at the launch.
  3. What makes the Oppo K13 series stand out?
    It features India’s first inbuilt cooling fan (18,000 RPM) and a 7,000 sq mm vapour chamber, perfect for gaming and multitasking.
  4. Where can I buy the Oppo K13 series?
    It will be available exclusively on Flipkart after the August 11, 2025 launch.
  5. What are the key specs of the Oppo K13 series?
    It includes a 7,000mAh battery with 80W charging, a 1.5K 120Hz display, a 50MP dual-camera, and processors like MediaTek Dimensity 8450 (K13 Turbo) and Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 (K13 Turbo Pro).
  6. What color options are available for the Oppo K13 series?
    Both models offer Purple Phantom and Midnight Maverick. The K13 Turbo Pro includes Silver Knight, while the K13 Turbo has White Knight.

Conclusion

The Oppo K13 Turbo 5G series, launching on August 11, 2025, brings cutting-edge features to India’s mid-range market. With its unique cooling technology, powerful performance, and sleek design, it’s a strong contender for gamers and tech enthusiasts. Priced under Rs. 40,000 and available on Flipkart, the K13 series is set to make waves. Stay tuned for the launch to confirm pricing and availability.
