Oppo F31 5G Series: India Launch on September 15, 2025 – Full Specs, Premium Features, Price, and Why It’s a Mid-Range Marvel

The smartphone market in India is about to get a major shake-up with the launch of the Oppo F31 5G series on September 15, 2025. Touted as the “Durable Champion,” this mid-range lineup from Oppo promises rugged durability, massive battery life, and stylish design at an affordable price. Comprising three models—Oppo F31 5G, Oppo F31 Pro 5G, and Oppo F31 Pro+ 5G—this series is set to redefine what budget-conscious buyers expect from a smartphone. With features like a 7,000mAh battery, IP66/IP68/IP69 ratings, and vibrant AMOLED displays, the Oppo F31 5G series is poised to compete with brands like Vivo, Realme, and Xiaomi in the ₹20,000-₹35,000 segment.

In this comprehensive guide, AlienWeb.in dives deep into the Oppo F31 5G series, covering its launch details, specifications, features, expected pricing, and why it’s a game-changer for Indian consumers. Whether you’re a student, gamer, or professional seeking a reliable yet stylish phone, here’s everything you need to know about the Oppo F31 5G series, launching in India on September 15, 2025.

Oppo F31 5G Series: A New Standard for Durability and Style

Oppo has officially confirmed that the Oppo F31 5G series will debut in India on September 15, 2025, at 12 PM IST. Marketed as the “Durable Champion,” the series emphasizes rugged build quality, long-lasting battery life, and a sleek design that stands out in the crowded mid-range market. The lineup includes three models: the Oppo F31 5G, Oppo F31 Pro 5G, and Oppo F31 Pro+ 5G, each tailored to different user needs while maintaining affordability.

The Oppo F31 5G series introduces a refreshed design with a textured rear panel, flat sides, and a circular camera module. Available in vibrant color options—Gold and Deep Blue for the base and Pro models, with additional Blue, Green, Red, White, and Pink variants across the lineup—these phones combine aesthetics with practicality. Oppo’s teaser campaign highlights the series’ “360-degree Armor Body” and ultra-slim design, making it both durable and comfortable to hold.

Launch Date and Availability in India

The Oppo F31 5G series is set to launch in India on September 15, 2025, at 12 PM IST, as confirmed by Oppo’s official X post. While the exact sale date hasn’t been announced, leaks suggest the series may go on sale shortly after, possibly by September 12–14, 2025, based on earlier rumors. The phones will likely be available through major e-commerce platforms like Flipkart and Amazon, as well as Oppo’s offline retail network across India, catering to both metro and tier-2/3 city buyers.

Oppo’s focus on affordability and accessibility ensures the Oppo F31 5G series will appeal to a wide audience, from students to working professionals. With its Made-in-India status, Oppo is also positioning the series for global exports, leveraging its robust manufacturing presence in India.

Expected Pricing: Affordable Mid-Range Power

Pricing is a key factor in the mid-range segment, and the Oppo F31 5G series delivers premium features at budget-friendly prices. According to tipsters like Yogesh Brar and Paras Guglani, here’s the expected pricing for the three models:

  • Oppo F31 5G: Under ₹20,000, making it cheaper than its predecessor, the Oppo F29 5G (₹23,999).
  • Oppo F31 Pro 5G: Around ₹25,000–₹30,000, offering a balanced upgrade.
  • Oppo F31 Pro+ 5G: Around ₹30,000–₹35,000, positioning it as the premium model in the lineup.

While exact storage variants and final prices are yet to be confirmed, these estimates place the Oppo F31 5G series in direct competition with brands like Realme, Vivo, and Xiaomi. The base model’s sub-₹20,000 price makes it an attractive option for budget buyers, while the Pro and Pro+ models cater to those seeking enhanced performance and features.

Oppo F31 5G Series Specifications: Power, Durability, and More

The Oppo F31 5G series is packed with features that balance performance, durability, and style. Here’s a detailed breakdown of each model’s specs, based on leaks and official teasers:

Oppo F31 5G: The Budget Powerhouse

  • Display: 6.57-inch AMOLED, 120Hz refresh rate, 2376 x 1080 pixels (1.5K resolution), offering vibrant visuals and smooth scrolling.
  • Processor: MediaTek Dimensity 6300 (6nm), ideal for everyday tasks, light gaming, and 5G connectivity.
  • RAM and Storage: 8GB RAM + 128GB storage (expandable via microSD up to 2TB), ensuring ample space for apps, photos, and videos.
  • Camera: Dual rear setup with a 50MP primary sensor and a 2MP depth sensor; 16MP front camera for selfies and video calls.
  • Battery: 7,000mAh with 80W SuperVOOC fast charging, reaching 50% in ~25 minutes.
  • Durability: IP66, IP68, and IP69 ratings for dust and water resistance, capable of withstanding submersion up to 1.5 meters for 30 minutes and high-pressure jets.
  • Operating System: Android 15 with ColorOS 15, offering a smooth, customizable interface.
  • Other Features: In-screen fingerprint sensor, dual SIM support, Bluetooth v5.4, 360-degree Armor Body design.
  • Color Options: Blue, Green, Red.

The Oppo F31 5G is designed for budget-conscious users who want durability and long battery life without breaking the bank. Its MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset ensures reliable performance for social media, streaming, and casual gaming.

Oppo F31 Pro 5G: A Step Up

  • Display: 6.57-inch AMOLED, 120Hz refresh rate, 2376 x 1080 pixels, matching the base model’s vibrant display.
  • Processor: MediaTek Dimensity 7300 Energy, offering improved efficiency and performance for multitasking and gaming.
  • RAM and Storage: 8GB RAM + 128GB storage (expandable), with virtual RAM expansion up to 16GB.
  • Camera: Dual rear setup with a 50MP primary sensor and a 2MP depth sensor; 32MP front camera for sharper selfies.
  • Battery: 7,000mAh with 80W SuperVOOC fast charging, ensuring all-day power.
  • Durability: IP66, IP68, and IP69 ratings, with the same rugged 360-degree Armor Body.
  • Operating System: Android 15 with ColorOS 15.
  • Other Features: In-screen fingerprint sensor, dual SIM, Bluetooth v5.4, stereo speakers.
  • Color Options: Gold, Grey.

The Oppo F31 Pro 5G targets users who want a bit more power and a better selfie camera, making it ideal for content creators and multitaskers.

Oppo F31 Pro+ 5G: The Premium Choice

  • Display: 6.79-inch AMOLED, 120Hz refresh rate, 1.5K resolution, offering a larger, immersive screen.
  • Processor: Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 3, delivering flagship-level performance for gaming and heavy apps.
  • RAM and Storage: Up to 12GB RAM + 256GB storage (expandable up to 2TB), perfect for media-heavy users.
  • Camera: Dual rear setup with a 50MP primary sensor and a 2MP depth sensor; 32MP front camera.
  • Battery: 7,000mAh with 80W SuperVOOC fast charging.
  • Durability: IP66, IP68, and IP69 ratings, with the 360-degree Armor Body design.
  • Operating System: Android 15 with ColorOS 15.
  • Other Features: In-screen fingerprint sensor, dual SIM, Bluetooth v5.4, enhanced cooling for gaming.
  • Color Options: Blue, White, Pink.

The Oppo F31 Pro+ 5G is the flagship of the series, offering top-tier performance and storage for users who want the best in the mid-range segment.

Why Durability Matters: The “Durable Champion”

The Oppo F31 5G series is marketed as the “Durable Champion,” and for good reason. All three models boast IP66, IP68, and IP69 ratings, making them resistant to dust, water submersion up to 1.5 meters for 30 minutes, and high-pressure water jets. Oppo claims the phones have been tested against 18 types of liquids, including coffee, juice, and soapy water, ensuring they can handle everyday mishaps.

The series also features a 360-degree Armor Body design, tested against seven MIL-STD-810H standards for extreme temperatures, dust, sand, and shock. With drop protection up to 1.8 meters, internal shock-absorbing pads, and reinforced corners, the Oppo F31 5G series is built to survive drops, spills, and tough conditions. This durability makes it ideal for students, travelers, and anyone prone to accidents.

Battery Life: A Game-Changing 7,000mAh

One of the standout features of the Oppo F31 5G series is its massive 7,000mAh battery across all models. This capacity is a significant upgrade over most mid-range phones, ensuring up to two days of usage on a single charge, even with heavy apps, gaming, or 5G connectivity. Paired with 80W SuperVOOC fast charging, the phones can reach 50% in about 25 minutes and a full charge in under an hour.

This battery prowess addresses a common pain point in India, where fluctuating 4G/5G networks can drain power quickly. Whether you’re streaming, gaming, or working on the go, the Oppo F31 5G series keeps you powered up without the need for a power bank.

Camera Capabilities: Capture Every Moment

Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the Oppo F31 5G series camera setups. All models feature a 50MP primary rear sensor paired with a 2MP depth sensor, delivering sharp, vibrant photos in various lighting conditions. The base Oppo F31 5G has a 16MP front camera, while the Pro and Pro+ models upgrade to a 32MP front camera for crisper selfies and video calls.

Features like Portrait mode, Super Night mode, and 2K video recording add versatility, making the Oppo F31 5G series a solid choice for social media enthusiasts and casual photographers. The circular camera module, a design highlight, adds a premium touch to the overall look.

Performance: Tailored for Every Need

The Oppo F31 5G series offers a range of processors to suit different budgets and performance needs:

  • Oppo F31 5G: MediaTek Dimensity 6300 (6nm) for reliable everyday performance.
  • Oppo F31 Pro 5G: MediaTek Dimensity 7300 Energy for improved efficiency and multitasking.
  • Oppo F31 Pro+ 5G: Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 for flagship-level gaming and app performance.

With 8GB RAM (expandable to 16GB via virtual RAM) on the base and Pro models, and up to 12GB RAM on the Pro+, the series ensures smooth multitasking. Storage options range from 128GB to 256GB, with microSD expansion up to 2TB, offering ample space for photos, videos, and apps. Running Android 15 with ColorOS 15, the Oppo F31 5G series provides a fluid, customizable interface with the latest features and security updates.

Color options add personality:

  • Oppo F31 Pro 5G: Gold, Grey.
  • Oppo F31 Pro+ 5G: Blue, White, Pink.

These vibrant choices cater to diverse tastes, from bold and playful to elegant and understated, making the Oppo F31 5G series a fashion statement.

Why the Oppo F31 5G Series Stands Out

The Oppo F31 5G series is more than just a smartphone lineup—it’s a bold step toward redefining the mid-range segment. Here’s why it matters:

Unmatched Durability

With IP66, IP68, and IP69 ratings and a 360-degree Armor Body, the Oppo F31 5G series is built to withstand India’s toughest conditions, from monsoons to dusty roads. This durability sets it apart from competitors like the Realme 15T or Vivo T4 5G, which often lack such robust ratings.

Massive Battery Life

The 7,000mAh battery is a game-changer, offering unmatched endurance for heavy users. Combined with 80W fast charging, it ensures you’re never tethered to a charger, even during long commutes or gaming sessions.

Affordable Pricing

Starting under ₹20,000, the Oppo F31 5G series delivers flagship-level features like AMOLED displays, 5G connectivity, and durable builds at a fraction of the cost of premium phones like the iPhone 17 or Samsung Galaxy S26.

Stylish Design

The textured rear panel, vibrant colors, and slim profile make the Oppo F31 5G series a head-turner. The circular camera module and flat sides give it a premium feel without the premium price tag.

Competing in India’s Mid-Range Market

The Oppo F31 5G series enters a competitive segment, facing off against brands like Vivo, Realme, and Xiaomi. Here’s how it compares:

vs. Vivo T4 5G

The Vivo T4 5G offers a similar price range (~₹20,000) and AMOLED display, but its battery is smaller (around 5,000mAh). The Oppo F31 5G series’ 7,000mAh battery and IP66/IP68/IP69 ratings give it an edge in durability and endurance.

vs. Realme 15T 5G

The Realme 15T 5G is a strong contender with a 120Hz AMOLED display and fast charging, but its battery (around 5,000mAh) and durability ratings don’t match the Oppo F31 5G series’ robust build and 7,000mAh capacity.

vs. Samsung Galaxy F36 5G

Samsung’s Galaxy F36 5G targets the ₹25,000–₹30,000 range, but its smaller battery and less comprehensive IP ratings make the Oppo F31 Pro 5G and Pro+ 5G more compelling for durability-focused buyers.

Who Should Buy the Oppo F31 5G Series?

The Oppo F31 5G series is ideal for:

  • Students: The large battery, durable build, and vibrant AMOLED display make it perfect for studying, gaming, and social media.

  • Budget Buyers: Starting under ₹20,000, it offers premium features like 5G, fast charging, and durability.
