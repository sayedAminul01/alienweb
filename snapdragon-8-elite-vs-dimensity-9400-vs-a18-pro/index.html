All the Details: Full Specs for Each Chip

Let’s explore Snapdragon 8 Elite vs. Dimensity 9400 vs. A18 Pro with every detail. Here’s what makes these chips tick.

Snapdragon 8 Elite

  • How It’s Made: TSMC 3nm (N3E), super tiny for great speed. It also saves battery life.
  • CPU (Brains): 8 cores—2 Oryon V2 Phoenix L at 4.32 GHz for gaming. Plus, 6 Phoenix M at 3.53 GHz for daily tasks.
  • Memory Boost: Bigger L2 and 50% bigger L3 cache for quick data. This keeps apps running smoothly.
  • Graphics: Adreno 830 supports Unreal Engine 5 and ray tracing. Snapdragon Game Super Resolution 2.0 ensures sharp visuals.
  • Gaming Tech: Adaptive Performance Engine 4.0 keeps games smooth. It’s perfect for long sessions.
  • AI Power: Hexagon NPU, 45% faster than the last chip. Great for photo edits, voice commands, or translations.
  • AI Features: Handles text, images, and voice AI. Makes tasks like selfie edits super quick.
  • 5G Modem: Snapdragon X80, up to 10Gbps download, 3Gbps upload. AI keeps signals strong in busy places.
  • Connectivity: Wi-Fi 7 (up to 5.8Gbps), Bluetooth 6.0 for fast pairing. Ultra-wideband (UWB) helps find devices like trackers.
  • Memory: LPDDR5X at 77 GB/s, up to 24GB. Switch apps super fast.
  • Camera Support: Spectra ISP handles 320MP cameras, 64MP+36MP dual setups. Supports 8K video at 60fps, 4K slow-mo at 120fps.
  • Camera AI: AI makes photos pop with better colors. Perfect for social media shots.
  • Display Support: Up to 4K+ Ultra HD at 144Hz or QHD+ at 240Hz. Supports HDR10+ and Dolby Vision.
  • Extras: Snapdragon Elite Gaming boosts games. AV1 video support and thermal tricks keep phones cool.
  • Phones Using It: Realme GT7 Pro, OnePlus 13, Samsung Galaxy S25 series.

MediaTek Dimensity 9400

  • How It’s Made: TSMC 3nm (N3E), just as efficient. Great for speed and power saving.
  • CPU (Brains): 8 cores—1 Cortex-X925 at 3.63 GHz for heavy tasks. Also, 3 Cortex-X4 at 3.3 GHz for performance.
  • More CPU: 4 Cortex-A720 at 2.4 GHz save battery. 6MB extra cache boosts speed.
  • Graphics: Immortalis-G925 MC12 with ray tracing. MediaTek HyperEngine 3.0 ensures smooth gaming.
  • Gaming Tech: Frame Rate Converter and Super Resolution for clear visuals. Great for budget flagships.
  • AI Power: NPU 890, 80% faster for AI tasks. It’s 35% better on battery.
  • AI Features: First chip for on-device video generation and LoRA training. Perfect for custom AI art.
  • 5G Modem: Integrated, up to 7.3Gbps download. AI keeps connections steady.
  • Connectivity: Wi-Fi 7 (5.8Gbps), Bluetooth 5.4. Supports dual 5G SIMs.
  • Memory: LPDDR5X at 77 GB/s, up to 24GB. Ensures smooth multitasking.
  • Camera Support: Up to 320MP cameras, 8K video at 30fps. 4K slow-mo at 120fps with AI.
  • Camera AI: AI improves low-light shots. Makes photos vibrant and clear.
  • Display Support: WQHD+ at 144Hz, 4K at 120Hz. Supports HDR10+, Dolby Vision.
  • Extras: HyperEngine boosts gaming. 50% bigger L3 cache and better cooling (avg. 43°C in Vivo X200 Pro).
  • Phones Using It: Vivo X200 Pro, OPPO Find X8, Redmi Turbo 4.

Apple A18 Pro

  • How It’s Made: TSMC 3nm (N3E), built to save power. Perfect for efficiency.
  • CPU (Brains): 6 cores—2 performance cores at ~4.05 GHz for fast tasks. 4 efficiency cores at ~2.2 GHz for lighter stuff.
  • CPU Style: Works best with iOS for smooth performance. Keeps apps running fast.
  • Graphics: 6-core Apple GPU, 20% faster than A17 Pro. 2x faster ray tracing for cool game visuals.
  • AI Power: 16-core Neural Engine, 35 trillion operations per second. Powers iOS features like Siri and photo editing.
  • 5G Modem: Integrated, up to 10Gbps download. Supports mmWave and sub-6GHz 5G.
  • Connectivity: Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4. Apple’s U1 chip for ultra-wideband tracking.
  • Memory: LPDDR5X, up to 8GB. Optimized for iOS efficiency.
  • Camera Support: Up to 48MP main camera with ProRAW. 8K video at 30fps, 4K cinematic mode at 120fps.
  • Camera AI: AI makes photos amazing. Perfect for Instagram-worthy shots.
  • Display Support: Up to 4K at 120Hz, ProMotion (1-120Hz refresh). Supports HDR10, Dolby Vision.
  • Extras: iOS-optimized speed, Metal API for gaming. Low power use (12W in multi-core tests) with great cooling.
  • Phones Using It: iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 16 Pro Max.

What’s the Vibe?: In Snapdragon 8 Elite vs. Dimensity 9400 vs. A18 Pro, the Snapdragon 8 Elite is a rocket for speed. The Dimensity 9400 is a smart all-rounder with AI tricks. The A18 Pro is a smooth cruiser for iPhone fans.
