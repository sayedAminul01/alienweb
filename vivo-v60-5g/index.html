Vivo V60 5G Cost in India

Vivo is prepared to introduce the Vivo V60 5G, its next-generation smartphone, following the success of the Vivo V50 5G series. The gadget, which is anticipated to make its Indian debut on August 12, 2025, may be a rebranded Vivo S30 from China.

The Vivo V60 5G is anticipated to retail for between ₹37,000 and ₹40,000 in India, according to numerous leaks, making it a strong contender in the high-end mid-range smartphone market.

The expected Coast Vivo V60 5G

With more storage and RAM options potentially costing up to ₹40,000, the Vivo V60 5G is anticipated to start at ₹37,000 in India. The phone’s price offers it up against well-known competitors like Xiaomi’s Redmi Note 13 Pro+, OnePlus Nord 4, and Realme 15 Pro 5G.

The expected price range is between ₹37,000 and ₹40,000.

RAM of 128GB plus 8GB

256GB + 8GB/12GB of RAM

Design and Display

The advanced 6.67-inch AMOLED flat screen of the Vivo V60 5G will provide:

Resolution of 1.5K (1260×2800 pixels)

120 Hz refresh rate

Peak brightness at 1300 nits (HBM)

HDR10+ and PWM dimming at 3840 Hz

A glossy textured rear panel in hues like Mist Grey, Moonlit Blue, and Auspicious Gold is part of the new design. According to reports, Vivo is moving away from curved displays in favour of flat panels for better style and usability.

Additionally, the phone will be certified IP68/IP69, which means it will be waterproof and dust-tight even when covered up to 1.5 meters and exposed to high-pressure water jets.

Hardware and Performance

The Vivo V60 5G will use the newest Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 (4nm) chipset to run. This powerful octa-core SoC is great for gaming, doing more than one thing at once, and doing advanced AI tasks.

Expected Specs:

CPU: 1×2.8 GHz + 4×2.4 GHz + 3×1.8 GHz

Adreno 722 is the GPU.

Storage for UFS 2.2

FuntouchOS based on Android 16

Vivo says it will give major Android updates for up to three years, which means long-term software support.

Charging and the Battery

One of the biggest 6,500mAh batteries in its class will be included with the Vivo V60 5G. It is acceptable with:

Fast charging with 90W wire (PD support)

Charging with reverse wire

Avoid charging while playing games.

This guaranteed constant use without the need for frequent recharging, from social scrolling to lengthy gaming sessions.

Details of the Camera

The Vivo V60 5G’s triple rear camera setup, which is branded with the ZEISS logo, is one of its best features:

50MP Primary Sensor (PDAF, OIS)

8MP Wide Angle Lens

3x Optical Zoom 50MP Periscope Telephoto Lens

The phone has a high-resolution 50MP front-facing camera for selfies and video calls, which can:

30 frames per second in 4K video

Pro-level clarity with Zeiss optics

HDR and advanced AI improvement
