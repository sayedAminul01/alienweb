On July 30, 2025, Motorola formally introduced the Moto G86 price in India, providing the ideal balance of innovation, style, and power in the mid-range market. The Moto G86 price Power is one of the most affordable smartphones of 2025, with a starting price of less than ₹20,000 in India. A 32MP selfie camera, a huge 6720mAh battery, a 50MP Sony LYTIA sensor, AI-powered camera tools, and military-grade IP68/IP69 durability are just a few of the features that make this device revolutionary for both performance-seekers and photography enthusiasts.

Price Moto G86

Moto G86 Power Price in India
The Moto G86 price in India is set at ₹17,999 for the 8GB RAM + 128GB storage variant. Buyers can avail up to ₹1,000 instant discount using select bank cards during the launch offer. The Moto G86 price and offer combination make it a value-packed choice for mid-range buyers. The device will go on sale starting August 6, 2025 via Flipkart, Motorola India website, and offline retail stores.

Launch Details

  • Date of Launch: July 30, 2025
  • Date of Sale: August 6, 2025, 12 p.m.
  • Availability: Motorola.in, Flipkart, and physical stores
  • There is a ₹1,000 bank discount available as an introductory offer.

Display

With its gorgeous 6.67-inch pOLED Super HD display, 1.5K resolution, and a maximum brightness of 4500 nits, the Moto G86 price justifies its mid-range tag with flagship specs. The screen is both smooth and durable, featuring a refresh rate of 120 Hz, HDR10+, and Gorilla Glass 7i protection.

OS and Performance

The MediaTek Dimensity 7400 chipset, 8GB of RAM, and 128GB of internal storage—which can be expanded to 1TB via microSD—power the Moto G86 price device. Hello UI, based on Android 15, further enhances the performance and user experience.

Battery

With a single charge, the 6720 mAh battery with 33 W TurboPower charging guarantees continuous use for more than 53 hours. For its Moto G86 price, this performance output is unmatched.

Camera

From selfies to cinematic videos, the Moto G86 price includes advanced camera features like:

  • Rear: 50MP Sony LYTIA 600 sensor + 8MP ultrawide/macro
  • Front: 32MP Selfie Camera with 4K Video
  • AI features: Magic Eraser, Super Zoom AI, Tilt-Shift Mode

Audio and Smart Features

The Moto G86 price not only ensures power and design but also immersive audio quality, thanks to dual stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos, Hi-Res Audio, and Moto Spatial Sound.

Connectivity

With support for 5G (11 bands), Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.4, NFC, and in-display fingerprint sensor, the Moto G86 price also includes Smart Connect 2.0 to easily connect with TVs and PCs.
