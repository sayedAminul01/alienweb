1. Photomath – The Most Popular Math Solver

Photomath is one of the most widely used tools among the Best AI Apps for Solving Math and Homework Problems. Students simply point their phone camera at a math problem, and the app instantly provides a solution with detailed steps.

Why students love Photomath:

– Step-by-step breakdown
– Camera scan feature
– Graph support
– Works offline
– Simple interface

Photomath is perfect for classwork, homework, and exam practice.

2. Socratic by Google – All-Subject Homework Helper

Socratic is a powerful study assistant and one of the Best AI Apps for Solving Math and Homework Problems. It covers math, physics, chemistry, history, biology, and more.

Key benefits:

– Visual explanations
– AI-powered homework solving
– Covers multiple subjects
– Works with pictures or typed questions

Socratic is ideal for students who want help beyond math.

3. Microsoft Math Solver – Strong Step-by-Step Solutions

Microsoft Math Solver is free, reliable, and one of the Best AI Apps for Solving Math and Homework Problems because of its detailed learning steps.

Features:

– Equation solving
– Graphs and visual representations
– Camera and handwriting input
– Supports algebra, calculus, statistics

Its accuracy makes it a favorite among high school and college students.

4. Brainly – Community + AI Homework Support

Brainly combines AI answers with community-based explanations. That’s why it belongs to the list of Best AI Apps for Solving Math and Homework Problems.

Why Brainly is powerful:

– Students and experts answer questions
– AI creates clear summaries
– Supports all subjects
– Fast, verified answers

It is perfect for doubt-solving and concept revision.

5. Mathway – Advanced Math Solver

Mathway has been helping students for years and remains one of the Best AI Apps for Solving Math and Homework Problems in 2026.

Why Mathway stands out:

– Solves basic to advanced math
– Instant answers
– Clean interface
– Great for competitive exam preparation

Whether it is algebra or calculus, Mathway handles it all.

6. Chegg Math & Homework Help

Chegg is a giant in the study support industry. Their AI-powered tools make Chegg one of the Best AI Apps for Solving Math and Homework Problems for step-by-step textbook solutions.

Best features:

– Homework explanations
– Study guides
– Chapter summaries
– Expert Q&A

Chegg is especially useful for college-level assignments.

7. Symbolab – Professional-Level Math Breakdown

Symbolab is a deep-level math solver and is praised as one of the Best AI Apps for Solving Math and Homework Problems among serious math students.

What Symbolab offers:

– Detailed step-by-step solutions
– Graphing
– Calculus, trigonometry, algebra
– Clean learning approach

Symbolab teaches you how the solution works—not just the final answer.

8. ChatGPT – Universal Homework Solver

ChatGPT is one of the most flexible tools in the list of Best AI Apps for Solving Math and Homework Problems.

Why ChatGPT is powerful:

– Solves math, science, English, history
– Gives explanations, not just answers
– Helps rewrite notes
– Improves clarity
– Great for last-minute homework

ChatGPT is the closest thing to having a personal tutor 24/7.

9. Khan Academy’s AI (“Khanmigo”)

Khan Academy apps are designed for learning deeply. Their AI tutor “Khanmigo” deserves a place among the Best AI Apps for Solving Math and Homework Problems.

Features:

– Practice sets
– Concept tutorials
– Smart hints
– Personalized study paths

It focuses strongly on understanding and long-term knowledge.

10. Quizlet AI – Homework Summaries & Quick Revision

Quizlet AI flashcards and summaries support students during homework and revision, making it one of the Best AI Apps for Solving Math and Homework Problems.

Why it’s useful:

– Creates flashcards from your homework
– Summaries difficult topics
– Memory-boosting tools
– Fast revision support

Great for exam week and last-minute preparation.

Are AI Homework Solvers Safe?

Yes, the Best AI Apps for Solving Math and Homework Problems are safe if used responsibly.
