CERTIFICATE IN TALLY.ERP9 ADVANCED

Duration: 4 MONTHS

Course Syllabus

Course Title: TALLY.ERP9 ADVANCED (Skills Development Course)

Course Duration: 4 Months (Part-time)

Fees: 4000.00

Course Objective:

The TALLY.ERP9 Advanced course aims to equip students with advanced skills in using Tally software for financial accounting, inventory management, taxation, and business management. The course is designed to enhance the learners’ employability and empower them for self-employment opportunities. By the end of the course, students will be able to proficiently use Tally.ERP9 to handle complex financial transactions, generate accurate financial reports, manage inventory efficiently, comply with taxation requirements, and make informed business decisions.

Course Overview:

This course provides in-depth knowledge and practical training on the advanced features of Tally.ERP9. It covers financial accounting basics, advanced financial accounting, inventory management, business management, document printing, data import/export, security, taxation including GST, and more. The course focuses on hands-on exercises and real-life scenarios to enhance practical skills and application of Tally software.

Course Teaching Methodology:

1.     Interactive Lectures: Engaging lectures delivered by experienced instructors to introduce and explain concepts.

2.     Practical Exercises: Hands-on practice sessions to apply the learned concepts in a simulated environment.

3.     Case Studies: Analyzing real-life business scenarios and using Tally.ERP9 to solve accounting and management challenges.

4.     Group Discussions: Collaborative discussions to encourage peer learning and exchange of ideas.

5.     Assignments: Regular assignments to reinforce understanding and practice advanced techniques.

6.     Project Work: Undertaking a comprehensive project to demonstrate proficiency in Tally.ERP9 advanced functionalities.

7.     Assessments: Periodic assessments to evaluate students’ progress and provide feedback.

Importance for Learners in Career Growth and Self-Employment:

1.     Enhanced Employability: Acquiring advanced Tally skills increases job prospects in accounting, finance, and business management sectors.

2.     Self-Employment Opportunities: Tally proficiency enables learners to start their own accounting or consulting businesses.

3.     Efficiency and Accuracy: Mastering Tally.ERP9 streamlines financial processes, improves accuracy, and saves time in record-keeping and reporting.

4.     Compliance with Taxation: Understanding taxation modules in Tally ensures compliance with relevant tax laws and reduces errors in tax calculations and filings.

5.     Decision-Making Abilities: Analyzing MIS reports and using Tally features for budgeting and scenario management aids in informed decision-making for business growth.
