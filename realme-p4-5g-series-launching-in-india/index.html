Realme P4 5G Series Launching in India on August 20 – Full Specs & Amazing Features

S Sayed Aminul
AlienWeb · Chauliabari, Bajali, Assam
Advertisement

The Realme P4 5G series, which will offer flagship features to the sub-₹30,000 market, will launch in India on August 20, 2025. The range includes the Realme P4 5G and Realme P4 Pro 5G, which are available for purchase on the Realme website and Flipkart.

With a 7000mAh battery, advanced cameras, and powerful CPUs, the P4 series is aimed for gamers, content creators, and consumers on a limited budget. It promises to transform the mid-range smartphone industry by utilizing cutting-edge technology with Indian-specific improvements.

Performance

The Realme P4 Pro 5G is powered by the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 CPU and HyperVision AI GPU for contemporary gaming and visual enhancements.

Important Facts to Keep in Mind:

50MP Sony IMX896 sensor (OIS), dual rear arrangement, 4K@60FPS & 4K HDR@30FPS recording, 50MP OV50D selfie camera, and AI enhancements.

6.77-inch HyperGlow AMOLED 4D Curve+ screen with 144 Hz refresh rate, HDR10+, and a maximum brightness of 6500 nits.

7000mAh battery with 10W reverse charging and 80W Ultra Charge (50% in 25 minutes).

Cooling: The CPU’s temperature can drop by up to 20°C with a 7000mm² AirFlow VC cooling system.

Playing: BGMI at 90 frames per second for about eight hours.

Its 7.68mm thickness makes it light and slender.

Dimensional Power & Visual Brilliance

The MediaTek Dimensity 7400 Ultra 5G chipset and a specialized Pixelworks processor are features of the regular Realme P4 5G that improve visual quality.

Crucial Things to Keep in Mind:

AI-enabled 4K video, 50MP main, 8MP ultrawide, and 16MP selfie cameras.

6.77-inch HyperGlow AMOLED screen with a maximum brightness of 4500 nits, HDR10+, and a refresh rate of 144 Hz.

The battery has a 7000mAh capacity, supports reverse charging, and has an 80W rapid charging capability.

Up to eleven hours can be spent playing BGMI.

Eye Comfort: TÜV Rheinland approved, hardware-level blue light suppression, 3840Hz PWM dimming.

Typical Elements That Set the Realme Series

Dual-Chip Architecture: The first in its class to employ both primary chipsets and specialized visual processors for less than ₹30,000.

Flagship-level displays: high refresh rates, ultra-high brightness, and pro-grade AMOLED with HDR10+.

Cooling Technology: For long-term performance, the largest vapor chamber cooling in the market.

Huge Battery Life: 7000mAh with reverse, bypass, and AI Smart Charging capabilities.

Features of AI Cameras: AI Motion Stabilization, AI Travel Snap, AI Landscape, and Ultra Steady Video.

Compersion on Realme P4 & Realme p4 pro

Realme aims to attract customers under ₹30,000. The P4 5G, which offers great value for performance, battery life, and display quality, may start at about ₹24,999, while the P4 Pro 5G is anticipated to cost closer to ₹28,999.

The Realme P4 Series’ Special Features
AI-driven features, longer battery life, and thermal management tailored for the Indian market are all highlights of the Realme P4 series. Its dual-chip design, pro-grade cameras, huge batteries, and brilliant displays make it an attractive choice for customers on a low budget seeking high-end functionality.

Regardless of whether you’re a normal user, gamer, or photographer, the Realme P4’s features offer great value, making its launch a must-see for 2025.

FAQs on Realme P4 Series

Q1: What is the Realme P4 series launch date in India?
A: The Realme P4 & P4 Pro 5G will launch on August 20, 2025 at 12 PM IST.

Q2: Which processor does the Realme P4 Pro 5G use?
A: It is powered by the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 paired with a HyperVision AI GPU.

Q3: How much battery capacity do these phones have?
A: Both models have a massive 7000mAh battery with 80W fast charging.
